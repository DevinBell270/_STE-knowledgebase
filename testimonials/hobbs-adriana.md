Provided by the WKU social media coordinator from a CEBS Student Ambassador Q&A.

Why do you want to be a teacher?

> I want to be a teacher for the students who need me the most. To me, elementary school is the foundation for a student's life. Early grades not only teach students how to read and write, but it also teaches them communication skills, self-love, accountability, and so much more. Students have so much going on between school and their personal lives, and I want to be someone they know they can come to if they need a smiling face, a hug, a shoulder to cry on or just someone to listen.

How is WKU preparing you for your future classroom?

> WKU has prepared me in ways I never expected. Not only have I been taught my "why" time and time again, but I also have learned strategies I never would have thought of. Western has taught me the inner psychology of teaching to retain information. Not only have I learned what to do, but I also have gotten to experience time in a real classroom setting to get exposure.
