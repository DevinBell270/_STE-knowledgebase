Provided by the WKU social media coordinator from a CEBS Student Ambassador Q&A.

Why do you want to be a teacher?

> I want to be a teacher because I think it would be amazing to watch someone learn and know that I was able to help them understand something they may have struggled with before. Being able to make a difference in a student’s life and be one of the reasons they succeed would be life-changing. I want my students to feel supported, encouraged, and confident in themselves, and I hope to create a classroom where they enjoy learning.

How is WKU preparing you for your future classroom?

> WKU has prepared me for my future classroom in so many ways. The sense of community on campus has helped me build meaningful connections with my professors and other students. My professors are always willing to help and genuinely want to see their students succeed. WKU also gives us opportunities to be in real classrooms and experience real situations, which has helped me understand what it takes to be a good teacher. These experiences are helping me build the skills and confidence I will need to create a positive and supportive classroom for my future students
