Provided by the WKU social media coordinator from a CEBS Student Ambassador Q&A.

Why do you want to be a teacher?

> I have wanted to be a teacher since I was 5 years old; I used to steal worksheets from my teachers’ recycling bins for my stuffed animal classroom! I want to become a teacher because I had such amazing teacher role models who showed me how to be a compassionate, genuine, and hardworking teacher. I want to give back to the community and schools that raised me.

How is WKU preparing you for your future classroom?

> WKU has prepared me for my future classroom in so many ways! You are in the classroom from your very first semester so you can see the end goal of working with kids from the start! I immediately felt support from the faculty and my professors; they do everything in their power to see me succeed! I love how they scaffold their Elementary and Special Education program so that you feel prepared to write lesson plans, make data-based decisions, and understand whole-brain teaching! With the help of my incredible professors at WKU, I feel supported and prepared to set up and manage my own future classroom soon!
