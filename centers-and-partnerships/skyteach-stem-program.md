# SKyTeach STEM Teacher Preparation Program

*School of Teacher Education (CEBS) & Ogden College of Science and Engineering*  
*Kelly Thompson Hall (KTH) 1011*  
*Western Kentucky University*

**SKyTeach** is Western Kentucky University's premier science and mathematics teacher preparation program, established in 2007 through a partnership with the National Math and Science Initiative (NMSI) and the UTeach Institute at the University of Texas at Austin.

Administered jointly by the **School of Teacher Education (CEBS)** and the **Ogden College of Science and Engineering**, SKyTeach enables undergraduate STEM majors to earn a full bachelor's degree in a STEM discipline alongside secondary teaching certification (Grades 8–12) within four years.

---

## The Master Teacher Concept

Unlike conventional teacher preparation programs where clinical courses are taught solely by university researchers, SKyTeach employs experienced, veteran P-12 public school math and science educators full-time as **Master Teachers**:

*   **Clinical Co-Director:** [[people/dr-martha-day|Dr. Martha M. Day, Ed.D.]] (Professor of Science Education)
*   **Master Teachers:**
    *   [[people/melanie-owens|Melanie D. Owens, M.A.E.]] (Mathematics Specialist)
    *   [[people/emily-perkins|Emily S. Perkins, B.S.]] (Middle Grades Science Specialist)
    *   [[people/mr-rico-tyler|Mr. Rico Tyler, M.S.]] (Physics & Astronomy Specialist)

Master Teachers teach the clinical pedagogical courses (SMED 101, 102, 320, 470), supervise candidates in public school classrooms, and provide one-on-one lesson planning mentorship.

## The SKyTeach STEM Curricular Sequence

```text
Freshman Year:     SMED 101 (Step 1: Elementary Inquiry) → SMED 102 (Step 2: Middle Inquiry)
                                     ↓
Sophomore Year:    SMED 310 (Knowing & Learning) → SMED 320 (Classroom Interactions)
                                     ↓
Junior Year:       SMED 340 (Perspectives) → SMED 360 (Research Methods Lab)
                                     ↓
Senior Year:       SMED 470 (Project-Based Instruction) → SEC 490 (Student Teaching)
```

## SKyTeach Workspaces and Facilities
*   **Kelly Thompson Hall (KTH) 1011:** Dedicated SKyTeach student workroom equipped with classroom science supplies, robotics kits, mathematics manipulatives, 3D printers, and collaborative lesson planning pods.
*   **STEM Teaching Resource Library:** Full collection of P-12 inquiry curricula, laboratory glassware, probeware, and graphing calculators available for candidate checkout.
