# Suzanne Vitale Clinical Education Complex (CEC)

*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Suzanne Vitale Clinical Education Complex (CEC)** at Western Kentucky University is a comprehensive, state-of-the-art clinical education and community service facility located at **104 Alumni Avenue** on the WKU campus. Established through private philanthropy and regional partnerships, the CEC integrates educational, clinical, and behavioral services for individuals diagnosed with autism spectrum disorders, developmental delays, speech/language challenges, and learning differences across the lifespan.

Directed by [[people/dr-christina-noel|Dr. Christina R. Noel, Ph.D., BCBA]], Professor of Special Education in STE, the CEC serves as an invaluable primary clinical training environment for WKU undergraduate and graduate teacher candidates.

---

## Core Clinical Units within the Complex

### 1. The Kelly Autism Program (KAP)
*   **Mission:** Providing lifelong educational, social, and vocational programming for individuals diagnosed with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).
*   **Programs:**
    *   *Elementary & Middle School Programs:* After-school academic tutoring, executive functioning coaching, and structured social skills groups.
    *   *High School & Transition:* Independent living instruction, community-based work experiences, and social transition clubs.
    *   *Circle of Support (College Program):* Comprehensive academic, mental health, and social coaching for neurodivergent college students attending WKU.
*   **Clinical Training:** Candidates in [[programs/graduate/applied-behavior-analysis-ms-0508|MS in Applied Behavior Analysis]] and [[programs/undergraduate/special-education-and-elementary-education-bs-5003|SPED Dual Major]] serve as paid tutors and clinical behavioral therapists under BCBA supervision.

### 2. Renshaw Early Childhood Center (RECC / "The Big Red School")
*   **Mission:** An inclusive, high-quality developmental preschool and early intervention center serving infants, toddlers, and young children with and without disabilities.
*   **Clinical Training:** Primary clinical observation and student teaching site for candidates in the [[programs/undergraduate/interdisciplinary-early-childhood-education-bs-526|B.S. in Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education (IECE)]] and [[programs/graduate/interdisciplinary-early-childhood-mat-0460|MAT in IECE]].

### 3. Communication Disorders Clinic
*   Diagnostic speech-language evaluations and therapy services conducted in collaboration with WKU's Department of Communication Sciences and Disorders.

### 4. Family Resource Center
*   Support groups, sibling workshops, advocacy consultation, and legal/IEP navigation resources for families across South-Central Kentucky.

---

## Integration with the School of Teacher Education

The CEC provides an unparalleled living laboratory for STE educator preparation:
*   **Supervised BCBA Fieldwork:** Fulfills clinical supervision requirements for graduate students completing the ABAI Verified Course Sequence in [[programs/graduate/applied-behavior-analysis-ms-0508|Applied Behavior Analysis]].
*   **Single-Subject & Applied Research:** STE faculty ([[people/dr-christina-noel|Dr. Christina Noel]], [[people/dr-ellen-casale|Dr. Ellen Casale]]) conduct cutting-edge research on behavioral interventions, peer-mediated social strategies, and assistive technologies.
*   **Practicum Placements:** Candidates complete specialized clinical field hours observing evidence-based practices in sensory integration, PECS, discrete trial training, and functional communication training.
