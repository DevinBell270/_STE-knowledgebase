# Centers and Partnerships Map of Content

This hub indexes the clinical complexes, specialized research centers, and public school district networks that enrich educator preparation in the School of Teacher Education.

---

## Specialized Centers & Facilities

*   [[centers-and-partnerships/suzanne-vitale-clinical-education-complex|Suzanne Vitale Clinical Education Complex (CEC)]]
    *   Comprehensive clinical facility housing the Kelly Autism Program, Renshaw Early Childhood Center, and developmental therapy clinics.
*   [[centers-and-partnerships/center-for-literacy|The Center for Literacy at WKU]]
    *   On-campus diagnostic reading clinic, community tutoring programs, and clinical reading practicum laboratory.
*   [[centers-and-partnerships/skyteach-stem-program|SKyTeach STEM Teacher Preparation Program]]
    *   UTeach replication initiative between CEBS and Ogden College featuring Master Teacher clinical instruction in Kelly Thompson Hall.
*   [[centers-and-partnerships/regional-school-district-partnerships|Regional School District Partnerships & Clinical Networks]]
    *   Collaborative agreements and clinical placement pipelines with Warren County Public Schools, Bowling Green Independent, Daviess County, JCPS, and regional partners.
