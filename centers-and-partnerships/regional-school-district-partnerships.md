# Regional School District Partnerships & Clinical Networks

*School of Teacher Education*  
*Western Kentucky University*

Western Kentucky University maintains deep, enduring collaborative partnerships with public school districts across South-Central Kentucky and the wider Commonwealth. These partnerships support high-quality pre-service field observations, co-teaching student teaching placements, Option 6 alternative certification hiring pipelines, and graduate educator rank progression.

---

## Core Primary District Partners

### 1. Warren County Public Schools (WCPS)
*   **District Profile:** One of Kentucky's largest and fastest-growing county school districts, operating over 25 elementary, middle, and high schools, including innovative magnets like the Beacon Academy and Warren County Area Technology Center.
*   **Collaboration:** Serves as the single largest clinical placement site for WKU undergraduate practicum students and student teachers.
*   **Hiring:** Employs hundreds of WKU graduates annually across all certification areas.

### 2. Bowling Green Independent School District (BGISD)
*   **District Profile:** An urban, high-performing independent public school district serving the central city of Bowling Green with an exceptionally diverse student body representing over 50 native languages.
*   **Collaboration:** Primary site for ESL/TESOL clinical placements, urban education seminars, and literacy practicums through Bowling Green High School and neighborhood elementary schools.

### 3. Daviess County Public Schools & Owensboro Independent Schools
*   **Location:** Owensboro, Kentucky (WKU Regional Campus hub).
*   **Collaboration:** Joint cohort delivery of elementary education and special education programs, supporting candidates in the Green River region.

### 4. Jefferson County Public Schools (JCPS) & Fayette County Public Schools
*   **Location:** Louisville and Lexington, Kentucky.
*   **Collaboration:** Active recruitment pipelines for high-need urban teaching vacancies, Option 6 special education teachers of record, and graduate rank change enrollment.

---

## Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) & Clinical Governance
All district clinical placements are governed by formal Memoranda of Understanding overseen by the [[overview/contact-and-facilities|Office of Professional Educator Services (OPES)]] in GRH 2011. District superintendents and curriculum directors serve on WKU's Teacher Education Advisory Council, ensuring continuous curricular alignment between university educator preparation and real-world district workforce needs.
