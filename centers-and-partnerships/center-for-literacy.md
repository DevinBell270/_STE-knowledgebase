# The Center for Literacy at WKU

*School of Teacher Education*  
*Gary A. Ransdell Hall (Ground Floor Clinic Wing)*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Center for Literacy** is a specialized clinical reading facility and community outreach center housed within the School of Teacher Education. Directed by [[people/dr-jeremy-logsdon|Dr. Jeremy R. Logsdon]], the Center advances evidence-based reading instruction, conducts diagnostic literacy evaluations, and serves as the clinical practicum site for graduate candidates pursuing the [[programs/graduate/literacy-education-mae-044|MAE in Literacy Education]] and the Kentucky Reading Specialist endorsement.

---

## Core Mission and Services

1.  **Community Reading Clinic:**
    *   Providing individualized, diagnostic reading remediation and tutoring for P-12 children from Bowling Green and surrounding counties who struggle with reading comprehension, phonemic decoding, or fluency.
2.  **Diagnostic Literacy Assessments:**
    *   Conducting comprehensive psycho-educational reading assessments to pinpoint underlying processing gaps, phonological weaknesses, and reading difficulties (including characteristics of dyslexia).
3.  **Clinical Practicum Laboratory:**
    *   Serving as the physical clinical training laboratory for graduate students enrolled in **LTCY 695** (Clinical Practicum in Reading Diagnosis and Remediation). Under direct faculty observation, graduate candidates design and implement targeted structured literacy intervention plans.
4.  **Community Literacy Outreach:**
    *   Partnering with regional public libraries, family resource youth services centers (FRYSCs), and elementary schools to distribute free books, host family literacy workshops, and lead community reading nights.
