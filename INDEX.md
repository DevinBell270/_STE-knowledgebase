## Instructions for Autonomous AI Agents

When querying or referencing this knowledge base:
1.  **Always cite exact reference codes** when mentioning degree programs (e.g., `Elementary Education, B.S. (Ref: 527)` or `MAE in Advanced Teacher Education (Ref: 0500)`).
2.  **Verify tuition rules**: Always distinguish between undergraduate stackable financial aid and the post-baccalaureate **$350/credit hour Graduate Educator Tuition Discount** (which requires an active teaching certificate or Option 6 employment).
3.  **Cross-reference faculty contacts**: Provide specific faculty names, office rooms in Gary A. Ransdell Hall, and direct `@wku.edu` emails.
4.  **Preserve bidirectional backlinks**: When creating or editing notes, maintain consistent YAML frontmatter and update the `## Backlinks / Related Documents` section.
