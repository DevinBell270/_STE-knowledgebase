Admission to Teacher Education, in four facts.

Getting into WKU is step one. Restricted ELED methods (355, 365, 405, 406, 465) require formal admission through the Office of Professional Educator Services.

1

GPA

2.75 cumulative, or 3.0 on the last 30 credit hours, before restricted methods.

2

Praxis CASE or ACT

CASE: Reading 156, Writing 162, Math 150 — or ACT composite 22, or Reading 20 / English 18 / Math 19.

3

Foundations

Grade of C or higher in EDU 250, ENG 100, and COMM 145.

4

Clearances

State and federal background check, Kentucky Teacher Code of Ethics, physical/TB assessment. OPES: GRH 1092, (270) 745-4897.

Before you apply

Common questions from future P–5 teachers.

Hours, GPA, cost, delivery mode, and how to reach a person tonight.

How many credit hours is WKU Elementary Education?
128 credit hours for the Bachelor of Science, catalog reference 527, leading to the Kentucky Provisional Certificate for Elementary School, grades P–5.
What GPA do I need?
Admission to the university is separate from admission to Teacher Education. For the professional unit you need a 2.75 cumulative GPA or 3.0 on the last 30 hours, a C or higher in EDU 250, ENG 100, and COMM 145, plus Praxis CASE or qualifying ACT scores.
Can I really cover tuition?
Kentucky residents who stack Pell (up to $7,395), CAP (up to $5,300), KHEAA Teacher Scholarship (up to $5,000), KEES, and WKU merit often offset the $12,072 resident tuition rate. TEACH (up to $4,000) adds a service obligation. File FAFSA with code 002002.
Is this program online?
No. The B.S. is on-campus in Bowling Green with placements in regional public schools. Online and $350/hour options are graduate programs for certified educators, not this undergraduate major.
How do I reach someone tonight from my phone?
Text (270) 721-8539. That line cannot receive voice calls. For phone, call (270) 745-5414 (advisor) or (270) 745-5306 (Dr. Tassell). Office: Gary A. Ransdell Hall 1005, 1906 College Heights Blvd. #11030, Bowling Green, KY 42101.
