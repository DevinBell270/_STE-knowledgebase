# Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame — 2025 Inductees

*Source: [CEBS News / WKU News](https://www.wku.edu/cebs/news/index.php?articleid=12788&view=article) — Friday, November 7, 2025*

## Summary

The **17th class** of the Governor Louie B. Nunn **Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame** was inducted at WKU on November 7, 2025. The Hall is housed in WKU’s College of Education and Behavioral Sciences in **Gary Ransdell Hall**. With this class, **50** educators have been inducted since 2008. WKU was chosen as the Hall’s home because of its century-long teacher-education legacy.

## 2025 inductees

* **Lois Chandler** (posthumous) — 56-year educator from Ballard County / LaCenter language arts lineage
* **Julie Cowan** — Kindergarten teacher, Adair County Primary School; Lindsey Wilson and **WKU** graduate
* **Jennifer Miller Fritsch** — Art teacher, Glasgow Middle School; **three-time WKU graduate**; WKU Gifted Studies collaborator

Ceremony speakers included President Timothy C. Caboni, CEBS Dean Dr. Corinne Murphy, Sen. Mike Wilson, Rep. Kevin Jackson, and a video message from Lt. Gov. Jacqueline Coleman.

More: [https://www.wku.edu/kythf/](https://www.wku.edu/kythf/)

## STE relevance

The Hall of Fame is a flagship CEBS/STE-adjacent public honor located in Ransdell Hall; inductees often include WKU alumni and partners in the regional clinical network.
