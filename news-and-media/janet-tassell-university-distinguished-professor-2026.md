# Dr. Janet Tassell Named University Distinguished Professor

*Source: [CEBS News](https://www.wku.edu/cebs/news/index.php?articleid=12919&view=article) — Friday, February 20, 2026*

## Summary

WKU President Timothy C. Caboni and the Board of Regents appointed **Dr. Janet Tassell** and **Dr. Monica Burke** as **University Distinguished Professors**, effective **July 1, 2026**. The University Distinguished Professorship is described as the highest faculty honor at WKU.

## STE connection — Dr. Janet Tassell

[[people/dr-janet-tassell|Dr. Janet L. Tassell]], Professor of Teacher Education, serves as **Assistant Director of the School of Teacher Education** and program director for multiple education programs. CEBS coverage highlights:

* Nearly two decades of research in mathematics education and gifted education
* Nearly **100** publications and **ten** peer-reviewed books
* About **$5 million** in external funding, including NSF awards and a competitive **Javits** award
* Work bridging educational technology and advanced learning

Dr. Monica Burke (Counseling & Student Affairs) was honored in the same announcement for inclusive pedagogy leadership (Inclusive Teaching Academy, ONE WKU, CITL).

Board meeting comments were pointed to WKU’s YouTube channel.
