# Six vs. Eighty-Six: STE Dual-Major Carly Wright

*Source: [WKU News / CEBS](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?articleid=13122&view=article) — June 1, 2026*

## Summary

**Carly Wright**, a dual-major in **Elementary Education** and **Special Education** (expected graduation May 2027), was featured for running literacy sessions for seniors at the Bowling Green Housing Authority while preparing for K–5 classrooms. She applies STE frameworks including scaffolding and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) across age groups and points prospective students to [wku.edu/ste](https://www.wku.edu/ste/).

## STE relevance

Student clinical/community story explicitly rooted in School of Teacher Education coursework and dual elementary/SPED preparation.
