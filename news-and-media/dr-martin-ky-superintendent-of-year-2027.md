# CEBS Alum Dr. Martin Named 2027 KY Superintendent of the Year

*Source: [@WKUCEBS on X](https://x.com/WKUCEBS/status/2088014236131758389) — August 13, 2026*

## Summary

A CEBS alum, **Dr. Martin**, was named the **2027 Kentucky Superintendent of the Year**. CEBS degrees cited:

* B.S. in Exceptional Education **LBD and MSD** (2008)
* M.A.E. in Educational Administration (2012)
* Ed.D. in Educational Leadership (2019)

The post linked a Meade County Schools article.

## STE relevance

Exceptional-education undergraduate pathway feeds STE’s special education pipeline; highlights long-term leadership outcomes.
