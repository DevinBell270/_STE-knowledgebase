# STE Professor Emeritus Dr. S. Kay Gandy Receives Fulbright Award

*Source: [WKU News / CEBS](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?articleid=13120&view=article) — May 28, 2026*

## Summary

**Dr. S. Kay Gandy**, Professor Emeritus in the School of Teacher Education, received a **2026–27 Fulbright U.S. Scholar** award in Education to teach at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Education**. This is Gandy’s **fourth** Fulbright. The award supports exchange of practices with pre-service teachers internationally.

## STE relevance

Direct STE faculty honor and international teacher-preparation collaboration.
