# Charley Jo Vaughn Joins STE as Instructor II

*Source: [@WKUCEBS on X](https://x.com/WKUCEBS/status/2087548804031553930) — August 12, 2026*  
*Continuation: [https://x.com/WKUCEBS/status/2087548805986017765](https://x.com/WKUCEBS/status/2087548805986017765)*

## Summary

**Charley Jo Vaughn** joined the **WKU School of Teacher Education** as **Instructor II**, teaching **SPED 335** and **EDU 360**. She is a double Hilltopper alumna from Franklin, KY. CEBS noted her great-grandmother attended the School of Teacher Education in the 1950s; Vaughn described herself as a fourth-generation Hilltopper continuing that teaching legacy.

Direct STE faculty hire and special education / foundations coursework.
