# Applications Open for Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame Class of 2026

*Source: [WKU News / CEBS](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?articleid=12993&view=article) — April 1, 2026*

## Summary

Nominations opened for the **Class of 2026** Governor Louie B. Nunn **Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame**, with a deadline of **August 15, 2026**. The Hall is housed in CEBS on the **first floor of Gary Ransdell Hall**; induction is expected later in 2026. Coverage notes WKU as Kentucky’s largest teacher-preparation program.

## STE relevance

Signature recognition program co-located with teacher education in Ransdell Hall.
