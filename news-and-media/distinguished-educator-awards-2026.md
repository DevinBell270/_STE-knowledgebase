# 6th Annual CEBS Distinguished Educator Awards (2026)

*Source: [CEBS News](https://www.wku.edu/cebs/news/index.php?articleid=13109&view=article) — Tuesday, May 19, 2026*

## Summary

WKU’s College of Education and Behavioral Sciences announced winners of its **6th annual Distinguished Educator Awards**, honoring exceptional P–12 educators across Kentucky. Regional districts nominate; superintendents submit entries. A selection committee of faculty from the **School of Teacher Education** and the School of Leadership and Professional Studies evaluates nominees.

Category winners receive a cash prize, commemorative plaque, and engraving on the permanent plaque in the **Louie B. Nunn Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame** in Gary Ransdell Hall. Photo/video highlights were directed to the CEBS Facebook page ([facebook.com/cebsatwku](https://facebook.com/cebsatwku)). Program info: [wku.edu/cebs/educatorawards.php](https://www.wku.edu/cebs/educatorawards.php).

STE faculty participate directly in selecting regional clinical partners and mentor teachers; awardees are engraved in the Hall of Fame space inside Ransdell Hall.
