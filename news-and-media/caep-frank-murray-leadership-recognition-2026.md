# WKU Team Receives CAEP Frank Murray Leadership Recognition

*Source: [WKU News](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?view=article&articleid=13273) — Thursday, October 1, 2026*

## Summary

A team of Western Kentucky University faculty and staff received the Council for the Accreditation of Educator Preparation (**CAEP**) **Frank Murray Leadership Recognition for Continuous Improvement**. CAEP awards it to educator preparation providers (**EPPs**) that achieved accreditation with no stipulations or areas for improvement by demonstrating strong evidence and data trends. Named after the founding President of the Teacher Education Accreditation Council (TEAC).

[[people/dr-corinne-murphy|Dean Corinne Murphy]] announced the award at a recent CEBS meeting, noting that faculty input, collaboration, and engagement led to a perfect accreditation report and this award.

The WKU News release names WKU’s accreditation team across CEBS, the School of Leadership and Professional Studies (SLPS), the School of Teacher Education (STE), Psychology, and the Associate Provost for Research:

*   [[people/dr-corinne-murphy|Dr. Corinne Murphy]], Dean, College of Education and Behavioral Sciences (CEBS)
*   [[people/dr-jennifer-klemm|Dr. Jennifer Klemm]], Associate Dean, CEBS
*   Lucas Green, Data Specialist, CEBS
*   Dr. Margie DeSander, Department Chair, School of Leadership and Professional Studies (SLPS)
*   Dr. Daniel Super, Associate Professor, SLPS
*   Dr. Stacey Leggett, Associate Professor, SLPS
*   Dr. Wes Cottongim, Assistant Professor, SLPS
*   [[people/dr-susan-keesey|Dr. Sue Keesey]], Department Chair, School of Teacher Education (STE)
*   [[people/dr-janet-tassell|Dr. Janet Tassell]], Professor, STE
*   [[people/dr-kandy-smith|Dr. Kandy Smith]], Associate Professor, STE
*   Dr. Sarah Bonis, Department Chair, Psychology
*   Dr. Jenni Redifer, Associate Provost for Research

More: [wku.edu/cebs](https://www.wku.edu/cebs)
