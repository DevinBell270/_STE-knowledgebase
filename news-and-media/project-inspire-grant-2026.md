# Project INSPIRE: $1,249,373 OSEP Grant for IECE–MSD Scholars

*Source: [WKU News](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?view=article&articleid=13260) — September 28, 2026*

## Summary

CEBS received a U.S. Department of Education **OSEP** grant for **Project INSPIRE**.

*   **Award:** **$1,249,373**
*   **Grant number:** **H325K260090**
*   **PIs:** [[people/dr-trudy-little|Dr. Trudy Little]] and [[people/dr-ellen-casale|Dr. Ellen Casale]]
*   **Scholars:** **24 undergraduate scholars** over **5 years**
*   **Pathway:** advanced **IECE–MSD** pathway; scholars **dual-enroll MAE coursework**
*   **IECE major:** expands IECE major content and fieldwork for children ages **0–5** with complex needs
*   **Goals named in the release:** certified personnel for young children with disabilities; evidence-based practices; community partnerships; Kentucky high-need workforce
*   **Program page cited:** [IECE](https://www.wku.edu/ste/iece.php)

## STE relevance

Named STE faculty and the IECE / MSD early-intervention teacher pipeline. This entry records the grant facts above only. It does not add program requirements, credit counts, or certification rules beyond what the WKU News release states.
