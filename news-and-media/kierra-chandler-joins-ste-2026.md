# Dr. Kierra Chandler Joins STE as Visiting Assistant Professor

*Source: [@WKUCEBS on X](https://x.com/WKUCEBS/status/2086840107705712926) — August 10, 2026*  
*Continuation: [https://x.com/WKUCEBS/status/2086840109727383901](https://x.com/WKUCEBS/status/2086840109727383901)*

## Summary

**Dr. Kierra Chandler** joined the **WKU School of Teacher Education** as a **Visiting Assistant Professor**, teaching **EDU 350** and **EDU 489**. A Nashville native and double WKU alumna, she taught in elementary classrooms for a decade and recently earned an **Ed.D.** at Carson-Newman University. She described returning to teach alongside former professors as a full-circle moment.

## STE relevance

Direct STE faculty appointment in educator-preparation courses.
