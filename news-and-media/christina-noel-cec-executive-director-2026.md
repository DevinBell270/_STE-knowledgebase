# Dr. Christina Noel Named Executive Director of Suzanne Vitale CEC

*Source: [WKU News / CEBS](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?articleid=13107&view=article) — May 18, 2026*

## Summary

**Dr. Christina Noel** was appointed **Executive Director** of the [[centers-and-partnerships/suzanne-vitale-clinical-education-complex|Suzanne Vitale Clinical Education Complex (CEC)]]. Coverage notes 20+ years of experience in autism and complex needs, more than **$1.5 million** in external funding, and a mandate to grow CEC as an interdisciplinary clinical and training hub (including units such as the Kelly Autism Program).

## STE relevance

CEC is a primary clinical partner for STE special education, ABA-adjacent, and related candidate experiences.
