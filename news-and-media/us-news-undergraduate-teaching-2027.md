# U.S. News 2027: WKU Undergraduate Teaching Programs No. 12 (South)

*Source: [WKU News](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?articleid=13252&view=article) — Tuesday, September 22, 2026 (CT)*

## Summary

Western Kentucky University was ranked in **13 areas** in *U.S. News & World Report*’s **2027 Best Colleges** rankings (released September 22, 2026). For the School of Teacher Education knowledgebase, the STE-relevant listing is:
