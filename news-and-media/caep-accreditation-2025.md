# WKU Education Programs Earn Full CAEP Accreditation

*Source: [CEBS News](https://www.wku.edu/cebs/news/index.php?articleid=12835&view=article) — December 4, 2025*

## Summary

WKU educator preparation programs earned **full CAEP accreditation** following an April **14–16, 2025** site visit. Accreditation runs through **December 2032** and covers initial and advanced licensure programs across CEBS and partner colleges (including OCSE, PCAL, and CHHS where applicable). More: [wku.edu/cebs/caep](https://www.wku.edu/cebs/caep).

## STE relevance

National quality seal for WKU educator preparation, including School of Teacher Education programs.
