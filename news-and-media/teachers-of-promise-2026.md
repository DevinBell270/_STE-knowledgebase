# WKU Students Named 2026 Teachers of Promise

*Source: [CEBS News](https://www.wku.edu/cebs/news/index.php?articleid=13119&view=article) — Wednesday, May 27, 2026*

## Summary

Four Western Kentucky University education students were honored at the **2nd Smoky Mountain Teachers of Promise Summit**, a regional celebration of exemplary future educators from Georgia, Kentucky, North Carolina, and Tennessee. The Teachers of Promise Foundation hosts the summit for professional learning and mentoring; the community has honored more than **4,000** future teachers since 2004.

## WKU honorees (2026)

* Samantha Boska
* Mikayla Gambrel
* Samantha Hartoin
* Sommers Smith

Photo credit on the CEBS release notes Dr. Julia Mittelberg with honorees Samantha Hartoin and Sommers Smith (Mikayla Gambrel and Samantha Boska also pictured).

## STE relevance

Recognizes WKU teacher candidates in the regional pipeline and reinforces CEBS/STE visibility among peer preparation programs.
