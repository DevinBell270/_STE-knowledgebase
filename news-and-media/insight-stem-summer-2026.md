# INSIGHT Summer STEM Innovation Program for K-12 Teachers

*Source: [WKU News](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?articleid=13116&view=article) — May 22, 2026*

## Summary

WKU launched the inaugural **INSIGHT** summer STEM innovation professional-development camp for K–12 teachers (**June 1–5, 2026**), supported by a **$100,000 TOP Grant**. CEBS is among the instructing colleges. Keynote: **Rico Tyler** ([[centers-and-partnerships/skyteach-stem-program|SKyTeach]]). Instructors include **Dr. Andrea Paganelli** among others; Center for Gifted Studies involvement noted in coverage.

## STE relevance

SKyTeach is STE’s STEM teacher pathway; the camp serves in-service teachers in the regional pipeline.
