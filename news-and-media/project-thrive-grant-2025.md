# Project THRIVE: $1.25M to Prepare Special Educators and School Psychologists

*Source: [WKU News / CEBS](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?articleid=12817&view=article) — November 20, 2025*

## Summary

The U.S. Department of Education awarded **$1,249,671** for **Project THRIVE**, which will train **20 graduate scholars** over **five years**. The project is a collaboration among **STE Special Education**, School Psychology, and the School of Leadership and Professional Studies.

* **PI:** Dr. Ellen Casale
* **Co-PI:** Dr. Sarah Bonis
* Leadership context includes CEBS Dean [[people/dr-corinne-murphy|Dr. Corinne Murphy]]

## STE relevance

Direct STE Special Education workforce-development grant.
