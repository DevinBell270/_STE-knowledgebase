# Incorporating Physical Education into Math (STE research video)

*Source: [WKU YouTube / WKUNews channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vTok1PcqYFA) — published May 14, 2026*
*Discovered via channel RSS: `https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCf8Wp9P8gWYrXV_d-LSOL1Q`*

## Summary

**Dr. YuChun “Jean” Chen** (Associate Professor in Physical Education) and **Jessica Hussung** (Instructor II, School of Teacher Education) researched how integrating physical education into math affects learning. The study compared math lessons at **Cumberland Trace Elementary** taught by a **pre-service teacher** and a mentor teacher with and without **TAGG-ED-integrated** activities.

## STE relevance

Direct STE faculty involvement (Hussung), pre-service teacher clinical research, and elementary math pedagogy — core School of Teacher Education territory.
