# The Center for Gifted Studies Hosts Over 400 Students at the 2026 Fall Super Saturdays

*Source: [WKU News](https://www.wku.edu/news/index.php?view=article&articleid=13322) — Tuesday, November 3, 2026*
*Dateline: November 2, 2026 (Bowling Green, Kentucky)*

## Summary

More than 400 students participated in the biannual Super Saturdays held by The Center for Gifted Studies on **October 17, 24, and 31** on the campus of Western Kentucky University (WKU). Students in **first through sixth grade** participated in one of **28 classes** for minds-on, hands-on learning led by educators from the southcentral Kentucky area.

For three decades, Super Saturdays has offered fall and winter programming in three-week sessions. The article describes the range students choose from as acting, missions in space, photography, math detectives, and robotics. That sentence is the article’s description of the program. **Jennifer Wilson**, program coordinator for Super Saturdays, is quoted on students arriving ready to learn, with examples of building a rocket, exploring cameras in photography, and learning about blood types. Those examples are in her quotation. A statement that each of those activities was a Fall 2026 class: **NOT FOUND IN SOURCE**.

Super Saturdays provides an opportunity for advanced learners and for families to learn how to support their student through the **Parent Seminars**. Workshops include topics such as the fundamentals of gifted education, advocacy, take-home enrichment ideas, and programs to challenge students. **Winter 2027 Super Saturdays** is scheduled for **February 6, 13, and 20**. Applications will open on **December 7 at 5:30 pm Central Time**.

For more information about Winter Super Saturdays 2027, the article says to contact Jennifer Wilson at [Jennifer.wilson@wku.edu](mailto:Jennifer.wilson@wku.edu) or call 270-745-6323.

## About The Center for Gifted Studies (as stated in the article)

The Center is located on the Western Kentucky University campus in Bowling Green, Kentucky. The article links [wku.edu/gifted](https://www.wku.edu/gifted/). It has been serving children who are gifted and talented, their educators, and their parents for more than 40 years. The Center provides educational opportunities for gifted young people, rigorous professional development for teachers, and support for parents of gifted young people. Under the direction of [[people/dr-julia-link-roberts|Dr. Julia Roberts]], the article says the Center has become one of the preeminent advocates for gifted education in the United States.
