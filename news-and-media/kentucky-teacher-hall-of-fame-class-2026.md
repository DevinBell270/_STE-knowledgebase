# Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame Class of 2026 Announced

*Source: [WKU News](https://www.wku.edu/news/articles/index.php?view=article&articleid=13276) — Friday, October 2, 2026*

## Summary

The **18th class** of The Louis B. Nunn Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame has been named. Joining in 2026, **Katherine Cleary**, **Lana Green-Willis**, and **Dorothy McCubbin** have been selected by a statewide committee. All three educators will be honored at the Hall of Fame ceremony on **Friday, December 4** on WKU’s campus.

*   **Mrs. Cleary** has taught at preschools and elementary schools in Monroe, Warren, Logan, and Allen counties over the past 23 years and counting.
*   **Mrs. Green-Willis** taught Social Studies at Grayson County High School for 32 years.
*   **Mrs. McCubbin** held a variety of educator and administrative roles in Barren County during her 37-year career and retirement.

Full biographies for all inductees will be released on December 4.

The Governor Louie B. Nunn Kentucky Teacher Hall of Fame was established in 2000 through a generous gift from former Governor Nunn, aimed at honoring the essential contributions of Kentucky’s classroom teachers and their positive impact on the state’s economy. **WKU was chosen as its home due to its century-long legacy in teacher education.** Learn more at [https://www.wku.edu/kythf/](https://www.wku.edu/kythf/).
