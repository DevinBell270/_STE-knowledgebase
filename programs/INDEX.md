# School of Teacher Education Academic Programs Matrix

This matrix provides a comprehensive, centralized index of all degree, rank-advancement, and certificate programs offered by the School of Teacher Education at Western Kentucky University.

**Credit-hour totals and course sequences** are taken from the live WKU catalog (undergraduate site currently labeled **2026–2027**; graduate site **2025–2026** as published). Undergraduate degree totals use **Finish in Four** sample plans where published; otherwise **Program Requirements**. WKU requires a **minimum of 120** unduplicated semester hours for any baccalaureate degree ([Degree Requirements](https://catalog.wku.edu/undergraduate/academic-information/degree-requirements/)).
