# Artificial Intelligence and Educational Technology Leader, Certificate (Ref: 1796)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Artificial Intelligence and Educational Technology Leader Certificate (Reference # 1796)** is a forward-looking, 12-credit-hour graduate credential that prepares educators to integrate generative AI tools, digital media, and automated instructional systems safely, ethically, and effectively into P-12 classrooms and school district workflows.

Eligible for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]** and stackable into [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE 0500]].

---

## Curriculum (12 Hours)
*   **LITE 537:** Educational Technology Tools for Teaching and Learning (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 547:** Web Design and Educational Multimedia Development (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 550:** Artificial Intelligence in Education: Principles, Prompting, and Practice (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 560:** Ethical, Legal, and Strategic Leadership in Educational Technology (3 hrs)
