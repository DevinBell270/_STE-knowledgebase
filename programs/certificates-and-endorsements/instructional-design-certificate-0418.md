# Instructional Design, Certificate (Ref: 0418)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Graduate Certificate in Instructional Design (Reference # 0418)** is a 12-credit-hour online credential for educators, trainers, and instructional developers who want core competency in instructional systems design (ADDIE, SAM), multimedia development, and performance analysis.

Stacks directly into the [[programs/graduate/instructional-design-ms-0428|MS in Instructional Design (Ref # 0428)]] or [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE 0500]].

---

## Curriculum (12 Hours)
*   **ID 560:** Foundations of Instructional Design and Learning Theory (3 hrs)
*   **ID 570:** Needs Assessment, Job Analysis, and Performance Improvement (3 hrs)
*   **ID 575:** Multimedia Development and E-Learning Authoring Tools (3 hrs)
*   **ID 585:** Evaluation of Training and Educational Programs (3 hrs)
