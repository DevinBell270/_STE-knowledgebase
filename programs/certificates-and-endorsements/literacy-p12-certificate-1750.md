# Literacy P-12, Certificate (Ref: 1750)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Literacy P–12 Certificate (Reference # 1750)** is a 15-credit-hour graduate credential designed for certified teachers who wish to qualify for the **Kentucky Reading Specialist Endorsement (Grades P–12)**. Candidates gain advanced competence in reading diagnosis, structured intervention, and literacy coaching.

Eligible for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Graduate Educator Discount]]** and stackable into [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE 0500]].

---

## Curriculum (15 Hours)
*   **LTCY 519:** Foundations of Reading and Language Development (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 523:** Diagnostic Reading Assessments and Interventions (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 524:** Content Area Literacy Strategies (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 528:** Literacy Coaching and Leadership in Schools (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 695:** Clinical Practicum in Reading Diagnosis and Remediation (3 hrs)
