# Literacy P-12, Certificate (Ref: 1750)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Literacy P–12 Certificate (Reference # 1750)** is a 15-credit-hour graduate credential for teachers and students in education-related fields who are interested in adding to their knowledge of literacy education. Coursework includes literacy development and instruction, assessment and intervention of reading and writing, content area and disciplinary literacy, and diversity and equity in literacy education.

A certification examination is **not** a requirement to earn this certificate. The Kentucky State Department of Education does require a passing score on the PRAXIS II Assessment to earn the Reading P–12 Endorsement. The endorsement is only available to students with current teaching certificates. Contact the Office of Professional Educator Services for more information.

Eligible for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Graduate Educator Discount]]** and stackable into [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE 0500]].

## Curriculum (15 Hours)

Catalog [Program Requirements](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/literacy-p12-certificate/):

**Required courses (12 hours)**
*   **LTCY 519:** Literacy Development and Instruction (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 523:** Assessment and Intervention of Reading and Writing (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 524:** Content Area and Disciplinary Literacy (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 527:** Differentiation in Literacy Education (3 hrs)

**Elective (3 hours)**
*   Any 500-level or 600-level course with prefix LTCY, EDU, PSY, ELED, IECE, LITE, SPED, GTE, ID, and ENG, or other advisor-approved elective (3 hrs)
