# Elementary Math Specialization P-5, Certificate (Ref: 0485)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Elementary Mathematics Specialization P–5 Certificate (Reference # 0485)** is a 15-credit-hour graduate certificate designed to prepare elementary teachers as mathematics teacher leaders, numeracy coaches, and curriculum specialists. Successful completion leads directly to the **Kentucky Elementary Mathematics Specialist (KEM)** endorsement (Grades P–5).

This certificate is 100% stackable within the [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE in Advanced Teacher Education (Ref # 0500)]] and qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Graduate Educator Discount]]**.

## Curriculum (15 Hours)
*   **MATH 411G:** Problem Solving for Elementary Teachers (3 hrs)
*   **MATH 507:** Mathematics for Elementary Teachers from an Advanced Perspective (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 571:** Leadership, Math Coaching, and Mentoring in Elementary Mathematics (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 572:** Investigation of Number, Operations, and Algebraic Thinking (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 573:** Investigation of Geometry, Measurement, and Data Analysis (3 hrs)
