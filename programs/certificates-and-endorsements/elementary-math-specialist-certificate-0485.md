# Elementary Math Specialization P-5, Certificate (Ref: 0485)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Elementary Mathematics Specialization P–5 Certificate (Reference # 0485)** is a 15-credit-hour graduate certificate designed to give education professionals a deep and practical knowledge of the content and pedagogy of elementary mathematics. It further develops skills for working with other professionals to develop mathematical knowledge for teaching, team teaching, or co-teaching in P–5 settings.

The EMS P–5 Certificate may be earned stand-alone or incorporated into another graduate degree. The catalog notes this program is exempt from the maximum of 12 hours in the Duplication of Coursework policy; all 15 hours may be duplicated on one additional graduate degree at the same level.

This certificate is 100% stackable within the [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE in Advanced Teacher Education (Ref # 0500)]] and qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Graduate Educator Discount]]**.

The EMS Certificate does **not** automatically add an endorsement to a teaching license. Elementary Education certified/licensed teachers in Kentucky (and reciprocal-licensure states) who complete this certificate and want the Elementary Mathematics Specialist endorsement added must contact the WKU Office of Professional Educator Services (or the appropriate office in their home state).

## Curriculum (15 Hours)

Catalog [Program Requirements](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/elementary-math-certificate/):

**Required courses (9 hours)**
*   **ELED 571:** Leadership, Math and Technology Education (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 572:** Math and Technology Methods for Diverse Learners (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 573:** Math and Technology Assessment (3 hrs)

**Select 6 hours from the following:**
*   **MATH 411G:** Problem Solving for Elementary and Middle Grades Teachers
*   **MATH 507:** Number Systems for Elementary and Middle Grades Teachers
*   **MATH 508:** Rational Number Concepts for Elementary and Middle Grades Teachers

Students must complete **MATH 411G** unless **MATH 411** was completed as an undergraduate. If MATH 411 was completed, both **MATH 507** and **MATH 508** must be completed.
