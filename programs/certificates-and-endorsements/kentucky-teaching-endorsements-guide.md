# Kentucky Teaching Endorsements Reference Guide

A Kentucky Teaching Endorsement is an official state credential attached to a base Kentucky teaching license that authorizes an educator to teach in specialized subject areas or assume instructional leadership roles. At Western Kentucky University, educators can earn state endorsements as standalone graduate certificates or **stack them directly inside a 30-hour master's degree** ([[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE 0500]]) at the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Educator Discount Rate]]**.

## Proficiency Evaluation Routes
Practicing teachers may also add secondary and middle school teaching subjects through Kentucky's **Proficiency Evaluation process** while completing an MAE:
*   Middle Grades Mathematics, Science, English, or Social Studies (Grades 5–9)
*   Secondary English, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Social Studies (Grades 8–12)
*   Learning and Behavior Disorders (KLBD) (Grades P–12)
