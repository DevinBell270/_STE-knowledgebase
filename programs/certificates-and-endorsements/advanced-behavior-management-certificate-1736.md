# Advanced Behavior Management, Certificate (Ref: 1736)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Advanced Behavior Management Certificate (Reference # 1736)** is a 12-credit-hour graduate certificate for general educators, special educators, school counselors, and administrators seeking deep competence in classroom management, multi-tiered systems of support (MTSS), and school-wide positive behavioral interventions and supports (PBIS).

Stacks directly into the [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE in Advanced Teacher Education]] or [[programs/graduate/special-education-lbd-mae-0457|MAE in Special Education]] at the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|discounted tuition rate]]**.

---

## Curriculum (12 Hours)
*   **SPED 530:** Advanced Behavior Management and Functional Behavior Assessment (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 531:** Advanced Assessment and Educational Programming in SPED (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 535:** Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS) and Behavioral Intervention (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 501 / SPED 518:** Applied Behavior Analysis Principles / Seminar in Behavior (3 hrs)
