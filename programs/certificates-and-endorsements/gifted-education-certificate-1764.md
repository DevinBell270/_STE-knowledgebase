# Gifted Education and Talent Development, Certificate (Ref: 1764)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Certificate in Gifted Education and Talent Development (Reference # 1764)** is a 12-credit-hour graduate credential designed to develop advanced certification for teachers who can positively impact student learning with all children and adolescents, including those who are gifted and talented. Courses and experiences include professional learning communities in which students interact with other graduate students from various content areas and grade levels.

Candidates who complete the Teacher Certification including a three-hour practicum and receive a passing score on Praxis II in Gifted Education will be eligible for a recommendation for the State of Kentucky Gifted and Talented Education (**KE37**) endorsement.

The certificate may also be completed by individuals in a variety of careers such as counselors, psychologists, international students, and others who are not certified teachers.

This certificate can be completed standalone or stacked within the [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE in Advanced Teacher Education]] or [[programs/graduate/gifted-education-mae-0482|MAE in Gifted Education]]. Qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Graduate Educator Discount]]**.

## Curriculum (12 Hours)

Catalog [Program Requirements](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/gifted-ed-certificate/):

**Required courses (6 hours)**
*   **PSY 432G:** Psychology of the Gifted and Creative (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 536:** Nature and Needs of Gifted, Creative, and Talented Students (3 hrs)

**Select 6 hours from the following:**
*   **GTE 537:** Curriculum, Strategies, and Materials for Gifted Students *(required for students pursuing Advanced Certification)*
*   **GTE 538:** Practicum for Teachers of Gifted Students *(required for Advanced Certification; restricted to students pursuing Advanced Certification)*
*   **GTE 539:** Assessment and Identification of Gifted and Talented Children
*   **GTE 540:** Developing Creativity and Leadership in Gifted Youth
*   **GTE 636:** Issues in Gifted Education and Talent Development
*   **GTE 637:** Seminar in Gifted Education and Talent Development

Admission: applicants must meet Graduate Studies admission requirements. A copy of the applicant’s teaching certificate or statement of eligibility must be submitted for students to be recommended for Advanced Certification. Applicants who are not pursuing Advanced Certification must contact the Program Coordinator to waive that admission requirement.
