# Gifted Education and Talent Development, Certificate (Ref: 1764)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Gifted Education and Talent Development Certificate (Reference # 1764)** is a 12-credit-hour graduate credential that fulfills state requirements for the **Kentucky Gifted and Talented Endorsement (Grades P–12) (Code: KE37)**. The program prepares teachers to coordinate gifted programs, differentiate instruction, and foster talent development in high-ability children.

This certificate can be completed standalone or stacked within the [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE in Advanced Teacher Education]] or [[programs/graduate/gifted-education-mae-0482|MAE in Gifted Education]]. Qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|Graduate Educator Discount]]**.

## Curriculum (12 Hours)
*   **GTE 536:** Nature and Needs of Gifted, Creative, and Talented Students (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 537:** Curriculum Differentiation and Instruction for Gifted Learners (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 538:** Practicum in Gifted Education and Talent Development (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 539:** Assessment and Identification of High-Ability and Underserved Gifted Learners (3 hrs)
