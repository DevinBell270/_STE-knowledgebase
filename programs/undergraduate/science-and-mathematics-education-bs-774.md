# Science and Mathematics Education (SMED / SKyTeach), Bachelor of Science (Ref: 774)

*School of Teacher Education (CEBS) & Ogden College of Science and Engineering*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Bachelor of Science in Science and Mathematics Education (Reference # 774)** is the professional education major for **SKyTeach**. Students seeking certification in middle grades (5–9) or secondary grades (8–12) must complete **both** SMED **and** one of: the middle school science (MSS) program (reference **734**), the middle grades mathematics (MGM) program (reference **730**), or a teacher-certifiable science or mathematics content major. This combination leads to a bachelor’s degree with a **minimum of two majors**.

Completion of both programs, plus current requirements to be recommended for certification, qualifies a student for Kentucky **middle grades science or mathematics certification (grades 5–9)**, or **secondary certification (grades 8–12)** in an approved content field. The catalog overview lists approved secondary fields as **biology, chemistry, earth and space science, mathematics, or physics**. Students seeking admission to SMED must earn a grade of **C or higher** in **SMED 101** and **SMED 102** and meet requirements for admission to teacher education.

## The SKyTeach Difference

*   **Try Teaching Early:** Candidates enroll in **SMED 101** (Step 1) and **SMED 102** (Step 2), planning and teaching inquiry lessons in school classrooms. Tuition for Step 1 is frequently reimbursed upon course completion (departmental practice; not a catalog hour requirement).
*   **Inquiry-Based Pedagogy (5E Model):** Focus on inquiry-based learning, student investigation, hands-on laboratory modeling, and the BSCS 5E Instructional Model (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate).
*   **Instruction by Master Teachers:** Clinical courses are taught by dedicated, veteran public school math and science teachers stationed in Kelly Thompson Hall (KTH 1011).

Recommended Colonnade mathematics (catalog): **MATH 117** or **MATH 118**.
