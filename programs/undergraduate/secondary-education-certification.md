# Secondary Education Teacher Certification (Grades 8-12 & P-12)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The live [School of Teacher Education undergraduate catalog page](https://catalog.wku.edu/undergraduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/) **does not list a single Secondary Education bachelor’s major** and **does not publish a Finish in Four professional education sequence** for secondary candidates. Secondary (grades 8–12), 5–12, and P–12 routes are completed as **content majors in other departments**, with advising in those departments **and** in STE (GRH 1005, 270-745-5414). Math and science teachers complete [[programs/undergraduate/science-and-mathematics-education-bs-774|Science and Mathematics Education (SMED), Ref. 774]] plus a content major.

Do not treat the former knowledge-base list (EDU 250, PSY 310, SPED 330, SEC 351, SEC 352, LTCY 421, SEC 453, EDU 489, SEC 490, plus a methods course) as a catalog-required STE sequence; that package is **not** printed as a program of study on the STE catalog page.

## Secondary Education (SEC) courses on the STE page

The STE catalog **Courses** inventory includes Secondary Education (SEC) courses (for example SEC 350 Clinical Practices in Secondary Teaching I; SEC 351 Teaching Strategies for Secondary Schools; SEC 352 Planning for Student Diversity; SEC 450 Clinical Practices in Secondary Teaching II; SEC 453 Management of Instruction; content methods such as SEC 473–483; **SEC 490** Student Teaching). That inventory is **not** a required 34-hour STE secondary major. Required professional education coursework for a given 8–12 or P–12 content major is published on **that content major’s** catalog page.
