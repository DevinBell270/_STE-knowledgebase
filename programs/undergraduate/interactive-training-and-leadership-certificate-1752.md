# Interactive Training and Leadership, Certificate (Ref: 1752)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Undergraduate Certificate in Interactive Training and Leadership (Reference # 1752)** is a **15-hour** credential. The instructional design process involves analyzing learning and performance needs and designing, developing, implementing, evaluating, and managing instructional and non-instructional solutions to improve performance in various settings. This program prepares students to analyze learners, learning contexts and skills, and design, evaluate, and revise learning activities, including multimedia training modules.

The catalog notes that training development is needed across workplace settings; graduates may produce print and electronic materials such as instructional videos, online activities, instructor manuals, product instruction manuals, professional development curricula, multimedia units, vocational/technical modules, classroom modules, and teaching aids.

*(STE catalog navigation sometimes labels this certificate **1725**; the program page title and Program Requirements heading use **1752**.)*

## Catalog competencies / learning outcomes

Students who successfully complete the certificate will:
*   Identify the steps, subordinate skills, and entry behaviors and knowledge required to accomplish an instructional goal and correlated performance objectives.
*   Produce an instructional system design process tailored to the education and training requirements of a specific organization or problem.
*   Design a training module for a specific organization or problem (context and learner analysis, objectives and test items, instructional strategies, revision from SME feedback).
*   Apply principles and strategies of multimedia design, interface design, and visual design to evaluating existing and creating new multimedia products in an instructional design setting.
*   Design, develop, and evaluate a multimedia module using appropriate software applications in training settings.
