# Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education, Bachelor of Science (Ref: 526)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The major in **Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education (Reference # 526)** requires catalog **Program Requirements of 81–89 hours** and leads to a Bachelor of Science degree. The major prepares early childhood educators to work with children, both with and without disabilities, ages **birth through kindergarten**, and their families. A grade of **C** or higher must be earned for all courses required for this major. No minor or second major is required.

Students select either the **teacher certification concentration** (leads to initial certification Birth–5 years) or the **non-certification concentration**. Graduates of the certification concentration are eligible to apply for certification in **IECE, Birth to Primary**, and are prepared for preschool and kindergarten positions in public schools, early intervention (First Steps), Head Start, and other agencies. Graduates of the non-certification concentration are prepared for preschool and kindergarten positions in private schools, Head Start, and other agencies, or as a First Steps Service Coordinator.

*(Catalog overview also states the major requires 69–76 hours, with certification at 75 hours and non-certification at 68 hours. The Program Requirements heading and Finish in Four totals are used below as the live requirement figures.)*

## Core Philosophical Foundations

The IECE program is built on four core commitments:
1.  **Family-Centered Practice:** Partnering with parents and caregivers as the primary decision-makers in young children's development.
2.  **Inclusive Early Education:** Embedding developmental interventions and positive behavior supports within natural, play-based learning environments.
3.  **Interdisciplinary Collaboration:** Working alongside speech-language pathologists, occupational therapists, physical therapists, and pediatric medical specialists.
4.  **Individualized Planning:** Developing Individualized Family Service Plans (IFSPs) for infants/toddlers and Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) for preschoolers.

Catalog program goals also emphasize current EC/ECSE research and recommended practices, lifelong learning through coursework and field experiences, culturally and linguistically responsive practice, and leadership in the EC/ECSE field.

## JUMP (Joint Undergraduate Master's Program)

The catalog offers JUMP so outstanding students may complete the B.S. in IECE and begin the **M.A.E. in Special Education: Moderate to Severe Disabilities (Ref: 0438)** in an accelerated timeframe. Up to **12** credit hours can be double-counted; up to **15** graduate hours may be taken while completing the undergraduate degree. JUMP courses listed: **SPED 529, SPED 535, SPED 610, SPED 615**.

Admission highlights (catalog): undergraduate IECE major; minimum **60** hours with at least **24** at WKU; at least **15** hours remaining on the bachelor’s; completed or enrolled in **15** IECE hours; cumulative GPA **3.25**; two letters from WKU IECE or SPED faculty and a statement of purpose.
