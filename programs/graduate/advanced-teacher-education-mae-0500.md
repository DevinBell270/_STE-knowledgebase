# Advanced Teacher Education, Master of Arts in Education (Ref: 0500)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Education (MAE) in Advanced Teacher Education (Reference # 0500)** is WKU's flagship graduate degree for certified teachers. Specifically engineered for maximum career return, this program allows educators to complete a 30-credit-hour master's degree, advance their Kentucky teaching rank from **Rank III to Rank II** (or from **Rank II to Rank I** if they already hold a master's), and simultaneously embed high-demand teaching endorsements and certificates into a single plan of study.

This program qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**.

## The "Stackable" Advantage: One 30-Hour Plan, Multiple Credentials

Rather than completing generic coursework, candidates in MAE 0500 customize their 30 hours to stack:
1.  **Conferred Master's Degree:** Master of Arts in Education from an accredited university.
2.  **Permanent District Salary Rank Advancement:** Jump from Rank III to Rank II (or Rank II to Rank I).
3.  **Kentucky State Endorsements:** Embed state-approved teaching credentials (Gifted, ESL, Elementary Math, etc.).
4.  **Dual-Credit High School Teaching Qualifications:** Allocate 18 graduate credit hours in a single content discipline (English, History, Math, Biology) to qualify to teach dual-credit college courses in high school.
5.  **Nationwide Reciprocity:** CAEP-accredited degree recognized across all 50 states under NASDTEC reciprocity.

## Degree Structure & Course Requirements (30 Hours)

The curriculum is divided into a Professional Education Core and an Area of Specialization / Concentration.

### 1. Professional Education Core (9–12 Hours)
*   **TCHL 500:** Foundations of Teacher Leadership (3 hrs)
*   **TCHL 530:** Curriculum Development and Evaluation (3 hrs)
*   **TCHL 555:** School and Classroom Assessment Strategies (3 hrs)
*   **TCHL 560 / EDU 595:** Action Research Capstone in Teacher Education (3 hrs)

### 2. Specialization / Concentration Options (18–21 Hours)
Candidates choose one of several high-impact concentration pathways:

*   **Elementary Mathematics Specialist (EMS):** 15 hours of math leadership coursework leading to the Kentucky KEM endorsement (see [[programs/certificates-and-endorsements/elementary-math-specialist-certificate-0485|Elementary Math Specialist Certificate]]).
*   **Gifted Education & Talent Development:** 12–15 hours leading to the Kentucky KE37 Gifted and Talented endorsement (see [[programs/certificates-and-endorsements/gifted-education-certificate-1764|Gifted Certificate]]).
*   **English as a Second Language (TESOL):** 12 hours leading to the Kentucky KE36 ESL teaching endorsement.
*   **Artificial Intelligence & Educational Technology Leader:** 12 hours leading to Certificate # 1796 (see [[programs/certificates-and-endorsements/ai-and-educational-technology-certificate-1796|AI Certificate]]).
*   **Advanced Behavior Management:** 12 hours leading to Certificate # 1736 (see [[programs/certificates-and-endorsements/advanced-behavior-management-certificate-1736|Behavior Management Certificate]]).
*   **Secondary Content Dual-Credit (18 Hours):** 18 graduate credit hours in English, History, Math, Biology, or Chemistry, satisfying SACSCOC faculty credentials for high school dual-credit teaching.

## Admission Requirements
*   Submission of Graduate School application.
*   Copy of a valid Kentucky teaching certificate (or out-of-state equivalent).
*   Official transcripts documenting a conferred bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75.
