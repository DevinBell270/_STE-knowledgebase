# Advanced Teacher Education, Master of Arts in Education (Ref: 0500)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Education (MAE) in Advanced Teacher Education (Reference # 0500)** is WKU's flagship graduate degree for certified teachers. Specifically engineered for maximum career return, this program allows educators to complete a master's degree (catalog **30–37 hours**), advance their Kentucky teaching rank from **Rank III to Rank II** (or from **Rank II to Rank I** if they already hold a master's), and simultaneously embed high-demand teaching endorsements and certificates into a single plan of study.

This program qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**.

The catalog program is divided into two components: **15 hours** of professional education (developed with the candidate's employing district) and a **concentration of 15–22 hours** in content, pedagogy, and/or professional growth. Programs are tailored to each student's interests. The degree can be used for a Rank change (for Kentucky teachers) and for pursuing additional certifications. This program is exempt from the maximum of 12 hours denoted in the Duplication of Coursework policy; all concentration hours may be duplicated on one additional graduate degree or certificate at the same level.

## The "Stackable" Advantage: One Plan, Multiple Credentials

Rather than completing generic coursework, candidates in MAE 0500 customize hours to stack:
1.  **Conferred Master's Degree:** Master of Arts in Education from an accredited university.
2.  **Permanent District Salary Rank Advancement:** Jump from Rank III to Rank II (or Rank II to Rank I).
3.  **Kentucky State Endorsements:** Embed state-approved teaching credentials (Gifted, ESL, Elementary Math, Literacy, Instructional Computer Technology, and others listed below).
4.  **Dual-Credit High School Teaching Qualifications:** Catalog note: **18 hours in a content discipline** are required for credentialing to teach at the postsecondary level.
5.  **Nationwide Reciprocity:** CAEP-accredited degree recognized across all 50 states under NASDTEC reciprocity.

## Degree Structure & Course Requirements (30–37 Hours)

During the program, students complete **15 hours** of school-district-driven coursework and a **minimum of 15 hours** of specialization (some concentrations are 12–16 hours and use electives to reach 30; TESOL is 16 hours; the catalog range is 30–37). Consult an advisor regarding coursework to meet professional goals.

### 1. Required Professional Education (15 Hours)
*   **EDU 502:** Positive Learning Environment (3 hrs)
*   **EDU 503:** Professional Development Experience (6 hrs) **or EDU 694:** National Board for Certification Exploration and Support
*   **EDU 580:** Advanced Special Topics (3 hrs)
*   **EDU 560:** Capstone in Education (3 hrs)

### 2. Concentration Courses (15–22 Hours)
For concentrations under 15 hours, students complete advisor-approved electives to meet the 30-hour degree minimum. Catalog electives may be selected from prefixes EDU, GTE, ELED, LITE, ID, IECE, SEC, LTCY, MGE, SPED, ENVE, TCHL, REC, CNS, SLP, SWRK, AGRI, AGEC, RSA, GEOS, HIST, ENG, PS, GEOG, PSY, BIO, CHEM, PHYS, FACS, ECON, EDAD, EDFN, LEAD at the 4XXG, 5XX, 6XX, or 7XX level (2–6 hours as needed). Additional program approval is required where noted in the catalog.

## Admission Requirements
Applicants must be accepted to WKU graduate studies and hold a valid teaching certificate. A copy of the certificate or statement of eligibility must be submitted with the application.

Applicants whose certificates have expired may be admitted, but they may enroll for only six hours before they must apply to the EPSB for re-issued certificates. After completion of six hours, a student admitted with an expired certificate must submit a copy of the re-issued certificate before being allowed to register for any additional courses.

Please refer to the admission section of the WKU graduate catalog for Graduate Studies admission requirements.
