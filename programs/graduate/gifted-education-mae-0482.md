# Gifted Education and Talent Development, MAE (Ref: 0482)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Education in Gifted Education and Talent Development (Reference # 0482)** is an internationally recognized graduate degree housed in WKU's endowed Center for Gifted Studies. Led by [[people/dr-julia-link-roberts|Dr. Julia Link Roberts]], the program prepares educators to identify, nurture, and challenge advanced, creative, and gifted learners across **Grades P through 12**.

Leads to the **Kentucky Gifted and Talented Endorsement (Grades P–12)** and qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**.

## Curriculum (30–37 Hours)
*   **GTE 536:** Nature and Needs of Gifted, Creative, and Talented Students (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 537:** Curriculum Differentiation and Instruction for Gifted Learners (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 538:** Practicum in Gifted Education and Talent Development (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 539:** Assessment and Identification of High-Ability and Underserved Gifted Learners (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 540:** Developing Creativity and Critical Thinking Skills (3 hrs)
*   **Teacher Leader Core & Electives:** 15 hours in teacher leadership, educational research (EDFN 500), and curriculum design.
