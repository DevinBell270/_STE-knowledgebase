# Elementary Education, Rank I (Ref: 084)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Elementary Education, Rank I (Ref: 084)** is a non-degree program that leads to eligibility for Rank I.

Catalog concentrations: Elementary Education (KEL); Elementary Math Specialist P-5 (KEM); Environmental Education (KEV); Gifted Education Endorsement (KE37); Kentucky Computer Technology (KCT); Teaching English as a Second Language Endorsement (KE36).

---

## Program Summary & Key Facts

| Feature | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Credential Type** | Non-Degree Planned Sixth-Year Program |
| **WKU Catalog Reference Number** | **084** |
| **Total Credit Hours** | **30 Hours** (30 semester hours in addition to a Planned Fifth-Year program, or 60 semester hours including a master's degree) |
| **Target Rank** | **Rank I** (Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board) |
| **Tuition Discount Eligibility** | **Eligible for $350/credit hour Graduate Educator Tuition Discount** |
| **Advising Unit** | School of Teacher Education (GRH 1005) |

---

## Curriculum (30 Hours)

Catalog [Program Requirements](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/non-degree-planned-sixth-year-elementary-rI/):

**Required Professional Education Component**
*   **EDU 601:** Seminar: Advanced Professional Development Plan (1 hr)
*   **ELED 603:** Advanced Seminar Elementary Curriculum (3 hrs)
*   **EDU 604:** Management Learning Environment (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 516:** The Exceptional Child: Perspectives and Issues for Educational Leaders (3 hrs) *(an approved elective may be substituted if an introductory Exceptional Education course is on the student's transcript)*
*   **EDU 698:** Seminar: Advanced Portfolio Project (2 hrs)

**Electives (approved by advisor)**
*   Professional education courses: 6–9 hrs
*   Specialization courses: 12 hrs

**Total Hours:** 30

## Comparison: Planned Rank Program vs. Stackable Master's Degree
As documented on the [[pathways-and-certification/rank-change-system|WKU Rank Change Comparison]], teachers are strongly encouraged to compare this non-degree option with WKU's stackable **MAE in Advanced Teacher Education (Ref # 0500)** or **Ed.S. (Ref # 0503)**. The stackable master's degree can require a similar credit load at the same **$350/hr** educator rate, achieves Rank change, and awards a conferred degree with nationwide portability.
