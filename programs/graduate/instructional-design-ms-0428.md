# Instructional Design, Master of Science (Ref: 0428)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Science in Instructional Design (Reference # 0428)** is a 30-credit-hour, 100% online degree that prepares professionals to analyze human performance gaps, design interactive digital learning interventions, develop multimedia e-learning courses, and evaluate training systems across business, healthcare, military, government, and educational organizations.

This program qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]** for certified teachers.

## Curriculum (30 Hours)
*   **ID 560:** Foundations of Instructional Design and Learning Theory (3 hrs)
*   **ID 570:** Needs Assessment, Job Analysis, and Performance Improvement (3 hrs)
*   **ID 575:** Multimedia Development and E-Learning Authoring Tools (3 hrs)
*   **ID 580:** Instructional Design Project Management (3 hrs)
*   **ID 585:** Evaluation of Training and Educational Programs (3 hrs)
*   **ID 590:** Interactive Simulation, Virtual Worlds, and Gamification (3 hrs)
*   **Approved Electives:** 9 hours selected from educational technology, AI in education, or business leadership.
*   **ID 595:** Instructional Design Capstone Practicum / Portfolio (3 hrs)
