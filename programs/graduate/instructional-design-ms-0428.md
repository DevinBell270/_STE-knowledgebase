# Instructional Design, Master of Science (Ref: 0428)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Science in Instructional Design (Reference # 0428)** prepares practitioners to assume leadership roles in public or private organizations. Instructional design is the systematic analysis of learning and performance needs, and the development of effective strategies, processes, systems, and products to address those needs. Program graduates are equipped to design and develop instructional elements such as instructional videos, online instructional activities, user manuals, professional development curricula, multimedia instructional units, training packs, vocational/technical instructional materials, lesson and unit plans, and educational curricula.

This program qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]** for certified teachers.

The curriculum for all Instructional Design courses is modularized (except for **ID 560** and **ID 570**). A student must demonstrate proficiency in each module. All courses will be offered for 3 hours credit (except for Special Topics and Practicum).

## Curriculum (30 Hours)

### Required Courses (18 Hours)
*   **EDFN 500:** Research Methods (3 hrs)¹
*   **ID 560:** Instructional Design Foundations (3 hrs)
*   **ID 570:** Systematic Instructional Design (3 hrs)
*   **ID 587:** Trends and Issues in Instructional Design (3 hrs)
*   **ID 590:** Practicum in Instructional Design (3 hrs)
*   **ID 595:** Internship in Instructional Design (3 hrs)

### Professional Emphasis Electives (12 Hours)²
Select 12 hours from the following:
*   **ID 575:** Special Topics in Instructional Design
*   **ID 590:** Practicum in Instructional Design
*   **ID 572:** Improvement Analysis in the Workplace
*   **ID 577:** Management of Instructional Systems
*   **ID 583:** Training Design and Development
*   **ID 588:** Multimedia Design
*   **ID 585:** Distance Education Opportunities and Challenges
*   With advisor approval, students may take up to 6 hours of adult education, educational technology, or other appropriate courses

¹ Or advisor-approved equivalent. Students may transfer in a previous graduate research course with a grade of B or higher that meets Graduate Studies Currency of Courses policy. If the course is over six years old, students may take a Research Competency Exam to satisfy this requirement and replace the 3 hours with an elective.

² The student and ID faculty advisor collaborate to select courses in the best interest of the student's career goals. Students must take 6–12 hours of Instructional Design courses from the professional emphasis list. If the 12 hours are completed in Instructional Design courses, no more than 6 hours between ID 575 and ID 590 courses may be taken. If a student elects to take 6 hours of advisor-approved adult education, educational technology, or transfer courses, then neither ID 575 nor ID 590 may be completed as part of the professional emphasis, and the student must complete two other ID professional emphasis courses listed above.

## Admission Requirements
Admission with a baccalaureate degree or master's degree requires:
1.  Bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited college or university with an overall undergraduate GPA of **2.75**; **or**
2.  Completed master's degree from a regionally accredited college or university with GPA of **3.0** or higher.
