# Certification Only, Teacher Education (Ref: 132)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

**Certification Only, Teacher Education (Ref: 132)** is a program for students seeking additional teacher certification. The certification/endorsement may be a stand-alone endorsement program or as part of a master’s degree in education; as an emphasis in a planned fifth-year (Rank II) or planned sixth-year (Rank I) program; or as a concentration in the Ed.S. degree.

Catalog heading: **Program Requirements (12–22 hours)**. Individual concentration tables range from 9–15 (Teacher Leader) to 25–27 (Library Media Education).

## Curriculum by concentration

Source: catalog [Program Requirements](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/certification-only/).

### Elementary Mathematics Specialist P-5, Endorsement (KEM) — 15 hours

*   **ELED 571:** Leadership, Math and Technology Education (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 572:** Math and Technology Methods for Diverse Learners (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 573:** Math and Technology Assessment (3 hrs)
*   Select 6 hours from: **MATH 411G** Problem Solving for Elementary and Middle Grades Teachers *(required unless MATH 411 was taken as an undergraduate)*; **MATH 507** Number Systems for Elementary and Middle Grades Teachers; **MATH 508** Rational Number Concepts for Elementary and Middle Grades Teachers *(MATH 507 and 508 required for students who completed MATH 411 as an undergraduate)*

### Environmental Education P-12, Endorsement (KEV) — 12 hours

*   **ENVE 520:** Introduction to Environmental Education (3 hrs)
*   **ENVE 580:** Instructional Strategies in Environmental Education (3 hrs)
*   **ENVE 560:** Investigating and Evaluating Environmental Issues (3 hrs)
*   Select 3 hours from: **ENVE 585** Special Topics in Environmental Education *(repeatable up to 6 hours)*; **GEOS 507** Geographic Concepts and Skills for Teachers; **REC 434G** Environmental Interpretation; other graduate courses chosen by the student with approval of the advisor (suggested from BIOL, EOHS, GEOS, PH, PS, SRSC, SUST)

### Gifted and Talented Education P-12, Endorsement (KE37) — 12 hours

*   **GTE 536:** Nature and Needs of Gifted, Creative, and Talented Students (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 537:** Curriculum, Strategies, and Materials for Gifted Students (3 hrs)
*   **PSY 432G:** Psychology of the Gifted and Creative (3 hrs)
*   **GTE 538:** Practicum for Teachers of Gifted Students (3 hrs)

### Instructional Computer Technology P-12, Endorsement (KCT) — 12 hours

*   **LITE 535:** Survey of Educational Technology Practices (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 537:** Educational Technology Management (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 508:** Informatics in Education (3 hrs)
*   Select 3 hours from: **LITE 545** Educational Technology Production; **LITE 547** Integration of Educational Technology; **LITE 550** Emerging Technology in Education; **LITE 737** Educational Technology Leadership

### Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education, Certification-Only (KIE) — 15 hours

*   **IECE 520:** Organizing Programs for Early Childhood Special Education (3 hrs)  
    *or* **SLP 517:** Normal Language Development  
    *or* **IECE 510:** Early Reading, Language, and Literacy
*   **IECE 521:** Assessment in Early Childhood Special Education (3 hrs)
*   **IECE 522:** Family-Focused Services (3 hrs)
*   **IECE 523:** Planning Curriculum and Instruction in Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 523:** Curriculum/Methods in Early Childhood Special Education (3 hrs)

### Learning and Behavioral Disorders, Certification-Only (KLBD) — 15 hours

Required for both tracks:
*   **SPED 530:** Advanced Assessment of Students with Disabilities in General Education (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 525:** Intervention Support for Students with Dyslexia and Other Reading Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 531:** Data-Based Individualization in Special Education (3 hrs)

Plus 6 hours:
*   Teachers without MSD or SpEd certification must take **SPED 630** Special Education Law and Finance and **SPED 517** Transition Services and Programs for Individuals with Disabilities
*   Teachers with MSD or SPED certification must take **EDU 502** Positive Learning Environment and **SPED 590** Advanced Internship in Special Education

### Library Media Education, Certification-Only (KLM) — 25–27 hours

*   **LITE 501:** Program Organization (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 506:** Organization of Information (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 508:** Informatics in Education (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 512:** Issues in Libraries, Informatics, and Technology in Education (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 518:** Advanced Children's Literature and Collections (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 527:** Advanced Young Adult Literature and Collections (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 535:** Survey of Educational Technology Practices (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 537:** Educational Technology Management (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 590:** Practicum in Libraries, Informatics, and Technology in Education (1–3 hrs)

### Literacy P-12, Certification-Only (ATEL) — 15 hours

*   **LTCY 519:** Literacy Development and Instruction (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 523:** Assessment and Intervention of Reading and Writing (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 524:** Content Area and Disciplinary Literacy (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 527:** Differentiation in Literacy Education (3 hrs)
*   Elective: any 500-level or 600-level course with prefix LTCY, EDU, PSY, ELED, IECE, LITE, SPED, GTE, ID, and ENG, or other advisor-approved elective (3 hrs)

### Moderate and Severe Disabilities, Certification-Only (KMS) — 15 hours

Required for both tracks:
*   **SPED 535:** Curriculum for Individuals with Moderate and Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 615:** Instructional Strategies for Students with Moderate and Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 529:** Assessment of Students with Moderate to Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)

Plus 6 hours:
*   Teachers without LBD or SpEd certification must take **SPED 630** Special Education Law and Finance and **SPED 517** Transition Services and Programs for Individuals with Disabilities
*   Teachers with LBD and SpEd certification must take **SPED 590** Advanced Internship in Special Education and **SPED 610** Characteristics of Low Incidence Disorders

### Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (TESOL), Endorsement (KE36) — 16 hours

*   **ENG 407G:** Linguistic Analysis (3 hrs)
*   **ENG 408G:** Psycholinguistics and Sociolinguistics (3 hrs)
*   **ENG 565:** Integrated Teaching English as a Second Language (3 hrs)
*   **ENG 566:** Seminar in Teaching English as a Second Language (3 hrs)
*   **ENG 471G:** TESL Practicum (4 hrs)

### Teacher Leader, Endorsement (KTLE) — 9–15 hours

*   **TCHL 500:** Foundations of Teacher Leadership (3 hrs)
*   **TCHL 530:** Curriculum Development (3 hrs)
*   **TCHL 545:** Classroom Instruction Strategies (0–3 hrs) *(complete the course or pass proficiency evaluations)*
*   **TCHL 555:** School and Classroom Assessment (0–3 hrs) *(complete the course or pass proficiency evaluations)*
*   **TCHL 560:** Capstone Project for Teacher Leadership (3 hrs)  
    *or* **EDU 560:** Capstone in Education

Students are expected to enroll in TCHL 500 at the beginning of the endorsement and in TCHL 560 or EDU 560 toward the end. Students must successfully complete TCHL 560 (course grade of "C" or higher) and present research results in an approved venue. Students must achieve a minimum 3.0 GPA overall and in endorsement course work.

## Comparison: Certification-only vs. stackable degree

This catalog program is for **additional certification or endorsement**, not a generic 30-hour Rank I plan. Teachers seeking Rank II/I plus a conferred degree should compare [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE 0500]] at the **$350/hr** educator rate.
