# Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education, MAT (Ref: 0460)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Teaching in Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education, Birth to Primary (Reference # 0460)** provides initial teacher certification for candidates holding a non-teaching bachelor's degree who wish to teach in early childhood and early intervention environments.

---

## Program Summary & Key Facts

| Feature | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Degree Awarded** | Master of Arts in Teaching (M.A.T.) |
| **WKU Catalog Reference Number** | **0460** |
| **Total Credit Hours Required** | **33–36 Hours** |
| **Kentucky Certification Issued** | Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education (Birth to Primary / Age 5) |
| **Option 6 Alternative Route?** | **YES** (Eligible for preschool teachers of record) |
| **Tuition Discount Eligibility** | Eligible for **$350/credit hour Graduate Educator Tuition Discount** |
