# Applied Behavior Analysis, Master of Science (Ref: 0508)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Science in Applied Behavior Analysis (Reference # 0508)** is an intensive, graduate professional degree that provides an **Association for Behavior Analysis International (ABAI) Verified Course Sequence (VCS)**. The program prepares graduates to qualify for and pass the national **Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA)** examination and practice in clinical, school, and community settings.

## Curriculum (30–33 Hours)
*   **EDFN 500:** Research Methods in Education and Behavioral Sciences (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 530:** Advanced Behavior Management and Functional Behavior Assessment (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 501:** Principles of Applied Behavior Analysis (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 502:** Single-Case Experimental Design and Behavioral Research (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 503:** Behavioral Assessment and Intervention Selection (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 504:** Ethics and Professional Conduct for Behavior Analysts (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 505:** Personnel Supervision and Organizational Behavior Management (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 506:** Advanced Concepts and Radical Behaviorism (3 hrs)
*   **ABA 590:** Supervised Fieldwork Practicum in Applied Behavior Analysis (3–6 hrs)
