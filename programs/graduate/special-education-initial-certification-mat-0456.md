# Special Education for Initial Certification, Master of Arts in Teaching (Ref: 0456)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Teaching: Special Education for Initial Certification (Reference # 0456)** is for individuals seeking **initial certification in Learning and Behavioral Disorders (LBD) or Moderate and Severe Disabilities (MSD)** who have **no prior teacher certification** in any area. Candidates may or may not be currently employed as a teacher of students with LBD or MSD.

This 33-hour program consists of coursework in induction to the profession of special education, diagnosis and assessment of disabilities, instructional strategies, data-based individualization, managing behavior and positive behavior supports, transition planning, and special education law. If a student is currently employed or has verification of employment from a school district, the student may be eligible for a Temporary Provisional Teaching Certificate (CA-TP). If the student does not secure employment as the teacher of record in an LBD or MSD setting during the program, the student will be required to complete the equivalent of the state required student teaching experience in an unpaid position in an accredited public school within WKU's service area. Graduates completing the equivalent of a student teaching experience will be eligible for a Statement of Eligibility for certification as a teacher of students with LBD or MSD.

This program is a primary pathway for Kentucky **Option 6 alternative certification** due to intense regional shortages of special educators.

Students must submit passing scores on the Praxis and be fully admitted to the professional education unit (teacher admissions) **prior to enrolling in SPED 590**. Students must meet all state requirements for student teaching and initial certification. **SPED 590 should be taken as the final course of the program.** Courses carry key assessments that must be completed before the final course grade will be recorded. To be recommended for teacher certification, students must document passing score(s) on the required Praxis examination(s).

## Curriculum (33 Hours)

### Required Core (18 Hours)
*   **EDU 502:** Positive Learning Environment (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 517:** Transition Services and Programs for Individuals with Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 630:** Special Education Law and Finance (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 618:** Social Skills Instruction & Behavioral Programming for Students with Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 590:** Advanced Internship in Special Education (6 hrs)

Students currently teaching in a classroom serving students with LBD or MSD (depending on concentration area) may use their current teaching position to meet the internship requirement with advisor approval. Students not currently teaching must complete an internship experience equivalent to state requirements for student teaching in a classroom serving students with LBD or MSD.

### Learning and Behavior Disorders Track (15 Hours)
*   **SPED 530:** Advanced Assessment of Students with Disabilities in General Education (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 531:** Data-Based Individualization in Special Education (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 525:** Intervention Support for Students with Dyslexia and Other Reading Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 515:** Introduction to Special Education (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 519:** Literacy Development and Instruction (3 hrs)

### Moderate and Severe Disabilities Track (15 Hours)
*   **SPED 535:** Curriculum for Individuals with Moderate and Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 615:** Instructional Strategies for Students with Moderate and Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 529:** Assessment of Students with Moderate to Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 610:** Characteristics of Low Incidence Disorders (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 612:** Collaboration between Schools and Stakeholders for Individuals with Disabilities (3 hrs)

## Admission Requirements
To be considered for admission, applicants must:
*   Document completion of a bachelor’s degree with a major, or equivalent, in an approved subject area and from an accredited institution.
*   Present a letter of application (including professional goals). Document a cumulative overall GPA of **2.75** or above (counting all course work, undergraduate or graduate) **OR** a GPA of **3.0** in the last 30 hours of course work (counting all course work, undergraduate and graduate).
*   Complete the departmental application (contact Christina Heady, christina.heady@wku.edu) including: references, physical including TB test, signed commitment to uphold the code of ethics, and criminal background check.

Please refer to the admission section of the WKU graduate catalog for Graduate Studies admission requirements.
