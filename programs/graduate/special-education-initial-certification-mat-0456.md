# Special Education Initial Certification (LBD), Master of Arts in Teaching (Ref: 0456)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Teaching: Special Education for Initial Certification in Learning and Behavior Disorders (Reference # 0456)** prepares candidates who hold a non-teaching bachelor's degree to become certified special education teachers for **Grades P through 12**.

Candidates are qualified to teach students with mild-to-moderate disabilities, including learning disabilities, ADHD, mild intellectual disabilities, and emotional/behavioral disorders. This program is a primary pathway for Kentucky **Option 6 alternative certification** due to intense regional shortages of special educators.

## Curriculum (33–36 Hours)
*   **SPED 515:** Introduction to Exceptional Education and Special Education Law (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 516:** Exceptional Child in the General Classroom (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 518:** Seminar: Contemporary Issues in Special Education (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 530:** Advanced Behavior Management and Positive Behavioral Supports (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 531:** Advanced Assessment and Educational Programming in SPED (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 532:** Instructional Methods for Learning and Behavior Disorders (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 533:** Curriculum and Instructional Adaptations for Special Populations (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 510:** Methods of Teaching Reading to Students with Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **Clinical Capstone:**
    *   Traditional: SPED 590 (Student Teaching in Special Education, 6 hrs)
    *   Option 6: SPED 599 (Alternative Route Internship Practicum, 6 hrs)
