# Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education, MAE (Ref: 0461)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Education in Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education (Reference # 0461)** is designed for certified early childhood educators who wish to expand their leadership capacity, advance their rank (**Rank II or Rank I**), and specialize in early intervention administration.
