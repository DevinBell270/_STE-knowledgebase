# Literacy Education, Master of Arts in Education (Ref: 044)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Education in Literacy Education (Reference # 044)** prepares certified teachers to serve as **Reading Specialists (Grades P–12)**, literacy instructional coaches, and curriculum leaders. Grounded in cognitive reading research and the science of reading, this 30-hour online/hybrid program integrates diagnostic reading assessments with clinical practicum experiences through WKU's on-campus [[centers-and-partnerships/center-for-literacy|Center for Literacy]].

Qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**.

## Curriculum (30 Hours)
*   **LTCY 519:** Foundations of Reading and Language Development (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 523:** Diagnostic Reading Assessments and Interventions (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 524:** Content Area Literacy Strategies (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 527:** Diversity and Cultural Linguistic Responsiveness in Literacy (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 528:** Literacy Coaching, Leadership, and Program Evaluation (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 624:** Seminar in Contemporary Literacy Research (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 695:** Clinical Practicum in Reading Diagnosis and Remediation (3 hrs)  
    *(Supervised clinical intervention at the [[centers-and-partnerships/center-for-literacy|Center for Literacy]])*
*   **Research & Elective Core:** 9 hours in teacher leadership, curriculum development, and educational research.
