# Teacher Education for Initial Certification, Master of Arts in Teaching (Ref: 0495)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) for Initial Certification (Reference # 0495)** is designed for individuals who hold a bachelor's degree in a non-teaching discipline (or a liberal arts/sciences content area) who wish to enter the teaching profession. This program leads directly to a master's degree and **initial Kentucky teacher certification** in:
*   **Elementary Education (Grades P–5)**
*   **Middle Grades Education (Grades 5–9)**
*   **Secondary Education (Grades 8–12)** in English, Math, Social Studies, Biology, Chemistry, Earth Science, or Physics.

This program offers two distinct clinical tracks: the **Traditional Route** (including student teaching) and the **Kentucky Option 6 Alternative Route** (employed full-time as the teacher of record while completing degree coursework).

## Two Completion Pathways

### Pathway A: Traditional MAT Route
*   Candidates complete professional education courses, pre-service clinical field hours, and a 16-week unpaid student teaching internship in an approved school district.
*   Ideal for career changers who want a gradual, mentored induction into the classroom.

### Pathway B: Option 6 Alternative Certification Route
*   Candidates secure a teaching position in a Kentucky public or accredited private school district.
*   The district and WKU submit an EPSB Option 6 mentoring plan and TC-TP form.
*   The state issues a **Temporary Provisional Teaching Certificate**, allowing the candidate to serve as the salaried, full-time teacher of record with full benefits while completing the MAT in 1–2 years.
*   See full details in [[pathways-and-certification/alternative-certification-option-6|Alternative Certification (Option 6)]].

## Curriculum Structure (30–36 Hours)

*   **EDU 520:** Planning for Instruction and Assessment (3 hrs)
*   **EDU 521:** Classroom Environment and Student Diversity (3 hrs)
*   **EDU 522:** Foundations of Differentiated Instruction (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 516:** Exceptional Child in the Regular Classroom (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 510 or LTCY 521:** Methods in Reading / Content Area Literacy (3 hrs)
*   **Specialized Methods Course:**
    *   Elementary: ELED 503 (Advanced Elementary Methods) (3 hrs)
    *   Middle Grades: MGE 575 (Middle Grades Curriculum and Methods) (3 hrs)
    *   Secondary: SEC 580 (Secondary Curriculum and Pedagogy) (3 hrs)
*   **Clinical Internship / Mentoring:**
    *   Traditional Track: EDU 589 (Seminar) + EDU 590 (Student Teaching, 6 hrs)
    *   Option 6 Track: EDU 591 / 592 (Option 6 Mentoring & Clinical Practicum, 3–6 hrs)

## Admission Requirements
1.  Conferred bachelor's degree from an accredited institution.
2.  Minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 2.75.
3.  Demonstrated content competency:
    *   For Secondary / Middle: A major in the teaching discipline, OR passing score on the required Praxis Subject Assessment (Praxis II).
    *   For Elementary: Broad liberal arts preparation meeting EPSB requirements.
4.  Clear background check and signed Kentucky Professional Code of Ethics.
