# Teacher Education for Initial Certification, Master of Arts in Teaching (Ref: 0495)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) for Initial Certification (Reference # 0495)** provides students with the pedagogical knowledge and skills to become highly qualified educators who will apply evidence-based teaching practices to increase student achievement. Graduates gain competencies in theory, research, and application of best teaching practices in their respective fields.

The degree offers four catalog concentrations:
*   **Elementary Education (EEED)** (grades K–5)
*   **Middle or Secondary Education (EMSE)** (grades P–12 or 5–9 or 8–12 depending on certification area)
*   **Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (ETES)**
*   **Secondary Science and Mathematics Residency (ERSM)** (grades P–12; cohort concentration)

Students in this program may or may not hold a temporary provisional teaching certificate. Program faculty advisors assist students in selecting coursework and culminating experiences. Contact the [[opes/INDEX|Office of Professional Educator Services]] for teacher certification information.

This program offers two distinct clinical tracks used in STE advising: the **Traditional Route** (including student teaching) and the **Kentucky Option 6 Alternative Route** (employed full-time as the teacher of record while completing degree coursework). Catalog internship credit is **EDU 589** (6 hrs) except in the SMED residency concentration (**SMED 590**, 8 hrs).

## Two Completion Pathways

### Pathway A: Traditional MAT Route
*   Candidates complete professional education courses, pre-service clinical field hours, and a student teaching internship in an approved school district.
*   Ideal for career changers who want a gradual, mentored induction into the classroom.

### Pathway B: Option 6 Alternative Certification Route
*   Candidates secure a teaching position in a Kentucky public or accredited private school district.
*   The district and WKU submit an EPSB Option 6 mentoring plan and TC-TP form.
*   The state issues a **Temporary Provisional Teaching Certificate**, allowing the candidate to serve as the salaried, full-time teacher of record with full benefits while completing the MAT.
*   See full details in [[pathways-and-certification/alternative-certification-option-6|Alternative Certification (Option 6)]].

## Curriculum Structure (30 Hours)

### Elementary Education Concentration (EEED)
*   **EDU 589:** Advanced Internship for the MAT (6 hrs)
*   **PSY 510:** Advanced Educational Psychology (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 515:** Introduction to Special Education (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 519:** Literacy Development and Instruction (3 hrs) **or LTCY 510:** Methods of Teaching Literacy to Adolescents
*   **ELED 503:** Organization of Elementary School Curriculum (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 505:** Mat/Meth/Math/El Teach (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 506:** Seminar in Elementary School Science (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 507:** Advanced Materials and Methods in Social Studies (3 hrs)
*   **ELED 509:** Advanced Methods and Materials for Elementary Education (3 hrs)
*   **Total: 30 hours**

### Middle or Secondary Education Concentration (EMSE)
*   **EDU 589:** Advanced Internship for the MAT (6 hrs)
*   **PSY 510:** Advanced Educational Psychology (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 515:** Introduction to Special Education (3 hrs)
*   **EDU 520:** Planning for Instruction (3 hrs) — should be completed within the first 9 hours
*   **EDU 522:** Foundations of Differentiated Instruction (3 hrs)
*   **EDU 570:** Educational Assessment for P-12 Learners (3 hrs)
*   **LITE 535:** Survey of Educational Technology Practices (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 510:** Methods of Teaching Literacy to Adolescents (3 hrs)
*   Choose one content-specific methods course (3 hrs): **SEC 534** Seminar in Mathematics Education; **SEC 535** Seminar in Music Education; **SEC 539** Seminar in Agriculture Education; **SEC 537** Seminar in Science Education; **SEC 538** Seminar in Social Studies Education; **SEC 546** Seminar in English Language Arts Methods; **SEC 573** Methods of Teaching Business and Marketing Education; **SEC 545** Investigations in Classroom Teaching. Other courses may be approved by advisor. Students may not duplicate courses from other completed programs.

### Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages Concentration (ETES)

*   **PSY 510:** Advanced Educational Psychology (3 hrs)
*   **LTCY 519:** Literacy Development and Instruction (3 hrs) **or LTCY 510:** Methods of Teaching Literacy to Adolescents
*   **ELED 503:** Organization of Elementary School Curriculum (3 hrs) **or EDU 520:** Planning for Instruction
*   **EDU 522:** Foundations of Differentiated Instruction (3 hrs)

*   **SMED 530:** Literacy Support for Diverse Learners in Mathematics and Science (3 hrs)
*   **SMED 520:** Management for Positive Learning Environments (3 hrs)
*   **SMED 560:** Developing Professional Learning Communities for Instructional Improvement (3 hrs)
*   **SMED 620:** Collaborative Research to Improve Mathematics and Science Teaching (3 hrs)
*   **SMED 630:** Action Research Seminar (1 hr)
*   **Total: 30 hours**

## Admission Requirements
*   Official transcript documenting an earned baccalaureate degree with a major in an approved certification area with a cumulative GPA of **2.75**.
*   Departmental application (contact Rachael Lee, rachael.lee@wku.edu) including: letter of application with professional goals; criminal background check; three letters of reference; physical examination (including TB test); signed code of ethics.
*   Meet required Praxis scores on the content major (**does not apply to elementary education**) — see www.ets.org.
*   Please refer to the admission section of the WKU graduate catalog for Graduate Studies admission requirements.
