# Gifted Education and Talent Development & Advanced Instructional Practice, Ed.S. (Ref: 0503)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Specialist in Education (Ed.S.) in Gifted Education and Talent Development & Advanced Instructional Practice (Reference # 0503)** is a prestigious, 30-credit-hour post-master's terminal degree. It provides educators who already hold a master's degree with a direct, research-focused pathway to achieve **Kentucky Rank I**, the highest standard teacher salary classification in Kentucky public school districts.

Qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**.

## Curriculum (30 Hours)
*   **Advanced Gifted & Instructional Core (15 hrs):** Advanced curriculum models, policy advocacy, program evaluation, and neuroscience of talent development.
*   **Specialist Research & Field Project (9 hrs):** EDFN 601, EDFN 603, and EDLD 699 (Specialist Project Capstone).
*   **Elective Specialization (6 hrs):** Tailored in educational technology, STEM pedagogy, or school administration.
