# Gifted Education and Talent Development & Advanced Instructional Practice, Ed.S. (Ref: 0503)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Specialist in Education (Ed.S.) in Gifted Education/Talent Development & Advanced Instructional Practice (Reference # 0503)** prepares leaders to research, understand, and implement strategies, procedures, and policies that allow children and youth with gifts and talents to achieve their potential. The program provides in-depth learning experiences to develop expertise in gifted education, talent development, and advanced instructional practices.

The program is divided into two instructional components. The first component is the **specialist project (6 hours, EDU 699 or EDAD 699)** and **investigative methodology (6 hours)** for **12 hours** of study. The second component is a **concentration** in specialist content. This program is exempt from the maximum of 12 hours denoted in the Duplication of Coursework policy. All concentration hours may be duplicated on one additional graduate degree or certificate at the same level. Concentrations that also lead to a certificate require a separate application for admission to the corresponding certificate program.

Qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**. Educators who already hold a master's degree may use the Ed.S. as a pathway to **Kentucky Rank I**.

## Curriculum

### Required Courses (12 Hours)
*   **EDU 699:** Specialist Project (6 hrs) **or EDAD 699:** Independent Specialist Project
*   Choose 6 hours of Investigative Methodology from: **GTE 636** Issues in Gifted Education and Talent Development; **GTE 637** Seminar in Gifted Education and Talent Development; **EDFN 500** Research Methods; **EDFN 501** Educational Statistics; **EDFN 603** Qualitative Research in Education; **EDFN 613** Action Research in Education Administration; **EDU 695** Advanced Topics in Education
