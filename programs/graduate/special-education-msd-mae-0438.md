# Special Education: Moderate and Severe Disabilities, MAE (Ref: 0438)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Education in Special Education: Moderate and Severe Disabilities (Reference # 0438)** offers courses and experiences for the advanced preparation of special education teachers who teach students with **low incidence disabilities**. The program prepares teachers to educate students with significant cognitive impairments, multiple disabilities, autism spectrum disorders, and complex behavioral challenges across **Grades P through 12**.

## Curriculum Outline (33 Hours)

This program focuses on assessment, instructional strategies, and/or areas of professional growth consistent with serving students with Moderate and Severe Disabilities.

*   **EDU 502:** Positive Learning Environment (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 517:** Transition Services and Programs for Individuals with Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 630:** Special Education Law and Finance (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 618:** Social Skills Instruction & Behavioral Programming for Students with Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 590:** Advanced Internship in Special Education (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 501:** Introduction to Applied Behavior Analysis (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 535:** Curriculum for Individuals with Moderate and Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 529:** Assessment of Students with Moderate to Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 615:** Instructional Strategies for Students with Moderate and Severe Disabilities (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 610:** Characteristics of Low Incidence Disorders (3 hrs)
*   **SPED 612:** Collaboration between Schools and Stakeholders for Individuals with Disabilities (3 hrs)

### Program Completion Requirements
Prior to earning certification, candidates must complete program requirements and meet Praxis exam scores (see www.ets.org).

## Joint Undergraduate Master's Program (JUMP)

The Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education program offers a JUMP pathway so academically outstanding students can complete a Bachelor of Science and partially complete this MAE in an accelerated timeframe. Up to **12 credit hours** can be double-counted, and up to **15 hours** of graduate courses can be taken while completing the undergraduate degree.

JUMP admission (catalog): undergraduate IECE major; minimum 60 hours with at least 24 earned at WKU; at least 15 hours remaining on the undergraduate degree; completed or enrolled in 15 credit hours in IECE; minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of **3.25**; two letters of recommendation from WKU IECE or SPED faculty; statement of purpose. JUMP courses: **SPED 529, SPED 535, SPED 610, and SPED 615**. See [[programs/undergraduate/interdisciplinary-early-childhood-education-bs-526|IECE B.S. (Ref: 526)]].

## Focus Areas
*   Systematic instruction and task analysis for life-skills acquisition.
*   Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) systems and assistive technology.
*   Transition planning from school to adult community and supported employment settings.
*   Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS) for severe challenging behaviors.
