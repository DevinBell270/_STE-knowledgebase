# Special Education: Moderate and Severe Disabilities, MAE (Ref: 0438)

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Master of Arts in Education in Special Education: Moderate and Severe Disabilities (Reference # 0438)** prepares teachers to educate students with significant cognitive impairments, multiple disabilities, autism spectrum disorders, and complex behavioral challenges across **Grades P through 12**.

## Focus Areas
*   Systematic instruction and task analysis for life-skills acquisition.
*   Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) systems and assistive technology.
*   Transition planning from school to adult community and supported employment settings.
*   Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS) for severe challenging behaviors.
