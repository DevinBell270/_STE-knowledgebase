# Alternative Certification: Kentucky EPSB Option 6 Route

*School of Teacher Education*  
*Western Kentucky University*

The **Option 6 Alternative Route to Certification** (authorized under Kentucky Revised Statute KRS 161.048 and 16 KAR 9:080) is an accelerated university-based pathway that allows an individual who holds a bachelor's degree in a non-teaching subject to be hired as the salaried, full-time **teacher of record** in a Kentucky public school district while concurrently completing a master's degree in teaching at WKU.

---

## How Option 6 Works: The Step-by-Step Pathway

```text
Step 1: Apply to WKU MAT Program (0495 or 0456) & Verify Content Eligibility
                                ↓
Step 2: Interview & Receive Formal Job Offer from Kentucky School District
                                ↓
Step 3: WKU & District Submit Form TC-TP to Kentucky EPSB
                                ↓
Step 4: State Issues Temporary Provisional Certificate (Valid for 1 Year, Renewable)
                                ↓
Step 5: Teach Full-Time as Teacher of Record + Complete MAT Evening/Online Coursework
                                ↓
Step 6: Complete Degree & Praxis Exams → Awarded Professional Teaching Certificate
```

---

## Option 6 Eligibility Checklist

To qualify for Option 6 admission and issuance of a Temporary Provisional Certificate, a candidate must:
1.  **Bachelor's Degree:** Hold a conferred bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university.
2.  **GPA Benchmark:** Minimum overall cumulative undergraduate GPA of **2.75** (or 3.0 on last 30 hours).
3.  **Content Verification:**
    *   *Secondary (8–12) / Middle (5–9):* Bachelor's degree in the subject area (e.g., Mathematics, English, Biology, History) OR a passing score on the state-required Praxis Subject Assessment (Praxis II).
    *   *Special Education (P–12 LBD):* Conferred bachelor's degree with 2.75 GPA.
    *   *Elementary (P–5):* Broad liberal arts coursework background.
4.  **Full-Time District Job Offer:** Provide an official offer of employment as a teacher in a certified teaching role from an accredited Kentucky public or private school.
5.  **University Mentoring Plan:** Formal agreement between the school district mentor teacher, district superintendent, and WKU clinical faculty supervisor.

## Significant Advantages of Option 6 at WKU
*   **Earn While You Learn:** Candidates draw a full-time, contracted teacher salary and health/retirement benefits from day one.
*   **Educator Tuition Discount:** Because Option 6 candidates hold a valid state-issued Temporary Provisional teaching license, they immediately qualify for the **[[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|$350/credit hour Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**.
*   **Job-Embedded Clinical Practice:** Instead of unpaid student teaching, daily classroom instruction in the candidate's own classroom counts toward clinical practicum credits.

## Comparison: Option 6 vs. Proficiency Evaluation Route

Western Kentucky University offers two distinct alternative routes to initial teacher certification:

| Dimension | Kentucky Option 6 (University-Based MAT) | Proficiency Evaluation Route (16 KAR 5:030) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Governing Statute** | KRS 161.048 (Option 6) / 16 KAR 9:080 | 16 KAR 5:030 (Proficiency Evaluation) |
| **Credential Conferred** | Master of Arts in Teaching (M.A.T.) Degree | Non-Degree Teaching Certificate |
| **Tuition / Program Structure** | Standard Graduate Tuition (Eligible for **$350/hr discount**) | **$500 Evaluation Fee** + Tailored Educator Learning Plan (ELP) |
| **Primary Requirement** | 30–36 graduate semester hours + job offer | Rigorous digital portfolio of evidence + school experience + 3-hr practicum |
| **Salary Rank Change** | Jumps directly to **Rank II** upon degree completion | Enters at **Rank III**; requires subsequent master's for Rank II |
| **Target Candidate** | Career changers seeking a permanent Master's degree | Experienced school paraprofessionals/teachers seeking fastest path |
| **Full Details** | See [[programs/graduate/teacher-education-initial-certification-mat-0495\|MAT 0495]] | See [[opes/proficiency-evaluations-alternative-route\|OPES Proficiency Evaluation Route]] |
