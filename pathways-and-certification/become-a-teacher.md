# Become a Teacher: The WKU Teacher Preparation Pipeline

*Western Kentucky University*  
*School of Teacher Education*

The **Become a Teacher** portal ([wku.edu/becomeateacher](https://www.wku.edu/becomeateacher/index.php)) is the primary recruitment and advisory gateway for prospective educators entering Western Kentucky University. Whether a high school senior exploring college majors, a community college transfer student, or a career changer holding a non-teaching bachelor's degree, WKU provides a supportive, clear, and clinically rich pathway into the classroom.

---

## The WKU Advantage: Why Prepare to Teach on the Hill?

1.  **Nationally Accredited, High-Value Credential:**
    WKU's teacher preparation programs are accredited by the **Council for the Accreditation of Educator Preparation (CAEP)** and approved by Kentucky's **Education Professional Standards Board (EPSB)**. A teaching degree from WKU carries immediate prestige across all 171 Kentucky public school districts and nationwide via interstate reciprocity.
2.  **200+ Hours of Real Classroom Experience:**
    Long before full-time student teaching, WKU candidates spend over 200 clock hours in local public school classrooms, working directly with mentor teachers, leading small-group interventions, and tutoring diverse learners (see [[pathways-and-certification/clinical-experiences-and-student-teaching|Clinical Experiences & Student Teaching]]).
3.  **Critical Regional Workforce Pipeline:**
    WKU is the primary supplier of certified classroom teachers across South-Central Kentucky and throughout the Commonwealth. Regional superintendents actively recruit on campus at annual education job fairs.
4.  **100% Tuition Coverage via Stackable Aid:**
    Through the strategic combination of federal, state, and institutional awards (KHEAA Teacher Scholarship, TEACH Grant, KEES, and WKU merit aid), eligible undergraduate education majors can see their tuition 100% fully covered (see [[funding-and-aid/teacher-funding-guide|Stackable Teacher Funding Guide]]).

## Career Outcomes & Regional Demand

### Projected Job Market Growth
*   **9.4% Projected Regional Growth** for education and instructional positions across Kentucky and the region *(Source: Bureau of Labor Statistics & Steppingblocks Graduate Insights)*.
*   Persistent state and national critical shortages in Special Education (LBD and MSD), Secondary Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, and Middle Grades.
*   **Freshman Recruitment Momentum:** Incoming freshman enrollment in teaching majors grew **+26.4%** from Fall 2024 (140 students) to Fall 2026 (**177 students** registering for TOP orientation), led by Elementary Education (115) and IECE (11) (see [[analytics-and-insights/enrollment-and-retention-trends|Recruitment Funnel Trends]]).

### Top Regional Employers of WKU Education Graduates
1.  **Warren County Public Schools** (Bowling Green, KY)
2.  **Bowling Green Independent School District** (Bowling Green, KY)
3.  **Daviess County Public Schools** (Owensboro, KY)
4.  **Jefferson County Public Schools (JCPS)** (Louisville, KY)
5.  **Fayette County Public Schools** (Lexington, KY)
