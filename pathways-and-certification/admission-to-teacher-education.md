# Admission to Teacher Education (Professional Education Unit)

*Office of Professional Educator Services (OPES)*  
*Gary A. Ransdell Hall, Suite 1092*  
*Western Kentucky University*

Formal **Admission to Teacher Education** is a state-mandated milestone required by the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board (EPSB) and Western Kentucky University's Professional Education Council (PEC). Students entering WKU as education majors begin in a "seeking admission" designation and must complete formal admission before enrolling in upper-division restricted teaching methods courses and clinical student teaching. Detailed step-by-step procedures, orientation dates, and forms are managed directly by [[opes/teacher-admissions|OPES Teacher Admissions]].

---

## Admission Criteria Checklist

Candidates must satisfy all of the following requirements before submitting their Teacher Education Application through the [[opes/overview-and-staff|Office of Professional Educator Services (OPES)]] in GRH 1092:

### 1. Academic Performance & GPA Benchmarks
*   **Minimum Cumulative GPA:** An overall cumulative GPA of **2.75 or higher** on all collegiate coursework; OR
*   **Last 30 Credit Hours:** A GPA of **3.00 or higher** on the last 30 credit hours attempted.
*   **Credit Hours Completed:** At least **30 semester hours** of undergraduate collegiate credit.

### 2. State-Approved Testing Requirements (EPSB)
Candidates must submit qualifying test scores through one of two pathways:

*   **Option A: Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators (CORE)**
    *   Reading (Test Code 5713): **Minimum Score 150**
    *   Writing (Test Code 5723): **Minimum Score 158**
    *   Mathematics (Test Code 5733): **Minimum Score 144**
    *   *Mandatory Codes:* Designate WKU (`1901`) and KY EPSB (`7283`) as score recipients.
*   **Option B: Qualifying ACT Scores (Subscores or Composite)**
    *   Composite Score of **22**; OR
    *   Individual Subscores:
        *   ACT Reading: **20**
        *   ACT English: **18**
        *   ACT Mathematics: **19**
*   *Note on SAT Equivalent:* Equivalent qualifying SAT scores are accepted in concordance with EPSB score charts.

### 3. Foundational Coursework Prerequisite
*   Completion of **EDU 250** (Introduction to Teacher Education) or approved equivalent introductory clinical course with a grade of **"C" or higher**.
*   Completion of College Composition (**ENG 100**) and Public Speaking (**COMM 145**) with grades of "C" or higher.

### 4. Background Clearances & Ethics Commitment
*   **State & Federal Criminal Background Check:** Active, unexpired national criminal background clearance (verified through Verified Credentials or approved district system).
*   **Child Abuse and Neglect (CAN) Check:** Clearance through the Kentucky Cabinet for Health and Family Services.
*   **Kentucky Professional Code of Ethics:** Signed declaration of adherence to the standards established under 16 KAR 1:020.
*   **Physical Exam & TB Screening:** Medical document on file confirming candidate is free of communicable tuberculosis.

## Step-by-Step Application Procedure

1.  **Meet with Advisor:** Consult with [[people/meredith-stewart|Meredith Stewart]] (GRH 1005) to review degree audits on TopNet and ensure testing/GPA prerequisites are satisfied.
2.  **Submit Electronic Application:** Complete the online Teacher Education Application portal hosted by OPES.
3.  **Audit & Committee Review:** The Office of Professional Educator Services verifies test scores, transcripts, background checks, and faculty dispositions.
4.  **PEC Approval & System Update:** Candidate is formally approved by the Professional Education Council and their TopNet major status changes from "Seeking Admission" to the official teacher certification major code.
