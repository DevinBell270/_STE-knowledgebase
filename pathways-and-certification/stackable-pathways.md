# Stackable Graduate Degree Pathways & Blueprints

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

A "stackable pathway" at WKU is a strategic academic roadmap that enables an educator to earn multiple credentials using the **exact same 30 credit hours**. Instead of completing random elective courses, every credit hour is intentionally aligned to satisfy requirements for a master's degree, a Kentucky salary rank change, and a state-recognized specialty credential.

All coursework qualifies for the **$350/credit hour [[funding-and-aid/educator-tuition-discount|WKU Graduate Educator Tuition Discount]]**.


## Featured 30-Hour Hour-by-Hour Degree Maps

### Blueprint A: High School Humanities Dual-Credit & ESL Specialist
*   **Base Degree:** [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE in Advanced Teacher Education (Ref: 0500)]]
*   **Total Hours:** Exactly 30 Graduate Credits
*   **Course Plan:**
    *   *Teacher Leader Core (6 hrs):* TCHL 500 (3 hrs), TCHL 560 Action Research (3 hrs)
    *   *Graduate Content Discipline (18 hrs):* ENG 501, ENG 502, ENG 504, ENG 507, ENG 514, ENG 520 (satisfies dual-credit 18-hour teaching standard)
    *   *TESOL Concentration (6 hrs):* ENG 565, ENG 566 (satisfies ESL Endorsement requirements when combined with content)
*   **Credentials Conferred:**
    1.  Master of Arts in Education
    2.  Kentucky Rank II or Rank I
    3.  Kentucky TESOL Teaching Endorsement (KE36)
    4.  SACSCOC Dual-Credit College Teaching Qualification

### Blueprint B: Elementary STEM Numeracy & Gifted Specialist
*   **Base Degree:** [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE in Advanced Teacher Education (Ref: 0500)]]
*   **Total Hours:** Exactly 30 Graduate Credits
*   **Course Plan:**
    *   *Teacher Leader Core (3 hrs):* TCHL 560 Action Research / Capstone (3 hrs)
    *   *Elementary Math Specialist Core (15 hrs):* MATH 411G, MATH 507, ELED 571, ELED 572, ELED 573 (Certificate # 0485)
    *   *Gifted Education Core (12 hrs):* GTE 536, GTE 537, GTE 538, GTE 539 (Certificate # 1764)
*   **Credentials Conferred:**
    1.  Master of Arts in Education
    2.  Kentucky Rank II or Rank I
    3.  Elementary Mathematics Specialist P–5 Endorsement (KEM)
    4.  Gifted & Talented Education P–12 Endorsement (KE37)

### Blueprint C: Special Education & Behavioral Interventionist
*   **Base Degree:** [[programs/graduate/special-education-lbd-mae-0457|MAE in Special Education: LBD (Ref: 0457)]]
*   **Total Hours:** Exactly 30 Graduate Credits
*   **Course Plan:**
    *   *Special Education Core (18 hrs):* SPED 515, SPED 516, SPED 531, SPED 532, SPED 533, SPED 590
    *   *Advanced Behavior Intervention (12 hrs):* SPED 530, SPED 535, ABA 501, SPED 518 (Certificate # 1736)
*   **Credentials Conferred:**
    1.  Master of Arts in Education
    2.  Kentucky Rank II or Rank I
    3.  State Teaching Certification in Exceptional Children: LBD (P–12)
    4.  Advanced Behavior Management Certificate (# 1736)
