# Kentucky Educator Rank Change System & Advanced Pathways

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

In the Commonwealth of Kentucky, public school teacher salary schedules are legally tied to an educator's recognized **Rank**, established by the **Education Professional Standards Board (EPSB)** under Kentucky Revised Statute (KRS 161.1211). Advancing one's rank is the single most powerful and permanent way for a Kentucky educator to increase lifetime earnings, pension calculations, and professional leadership opportunities.

---

## Understanding the Kentucky Rank Hierarchy

| Kentucky Rank | Definition & Qualifications | Typical Annual Salary Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rank III** | Holds a regular approved bachelor's degree and a valid Kentucky provisional teaching certificate. | Base district salary schedule |
| **Rank II** | Holds an approved **master's degree** in education or teaching subject; OR an approved **32-hour non-degree 5th-year planned program**; OR National Board Certification (NBCT). | **+$4,000 to +$7,000 / year** increase over Rank III |
| **Rank I** | Holds an approved **Specialist in Education (Ed.S.) degree**, doctorate, or **second approved master's degree**; OR a **30-hour non-degree 6th-year planned program** beyond Rank II (minimum 60 graduate hours total). | **+$8,000 to +$14,000 / year** increase over Rank III |

## The WKU Advantage: Maximize the Same 30 Hours

**"One Pathway. Multiple Credentials."**

Practicing Kentucky teachers must complete **30 graduate credit hours** to advance from Rank III to Rank II, and an additional **30 graduate credit hours** to advance to Rank I. 

Many teachers mistakenly enroll in generic, non-degree rank-change programs that award only a local salary bump. At WKU, our stackable graduate degree programs are engineered so that **the exact same 30 credit hours** achieve **five simultaneous milestones**:

1.  **Full Master's Degree:** Conferred M.A.E., M.S., or Ed.S. degree from an accredited university.
2.  **Permanent Kentucky Rank Advancement:** Immediate jump to Rank II or Rank I on district salary schedules.
3.  **State Teaching Endorsements:** Embed high-demand credentials (Gifted, ESL/TESOL, Elementary Math Specialist, Literacy, etc.) using concentration slots.
4.  **Dual-Credit Teaching Eligibility:** Allocate 18 graduate credit hours in a specific academic discipline (English, Math, History, Biology) to satisfy SACSCOC requirements for teaching dual-credit college courses in high school.
5.  **Nationwide Reciprocity & Mobility:** SACSCOC- and CAEP-accredited degree recognized across all 50 states for advanced salary placement (Master's / Master's + 30), transferring effortlessly if a teacher relocates out of state.
