# Stackable Teacher Funding & Financial Aid Guide

*School of Teacher Education*  
*Western Kentucky University*

At Western Kentucky University, aspiring teachers can take advantage of powerful financial aid programs that, when strategically combined, often **offset 100% of the cost of tuition**. The secret is **"stacking"** multiple independent sources of federal, state, teacher-specific, and university funds.

---

## How "Stackable" Aid Covers Full Tuition

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Tier 4: WKU Institutional & Departmental Awards       │ (Merit, CEBS, Foundation)
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Tier 3: Teacher-Specific Aid (KHEAA, TEACH, TRLP)     │ ($4,000 - $5,000 / year)
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Tier 2: Kentucky State Aid (KEES, CAP Grant)          │ (Up to $5,300 / year)
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Tier 1: Federal Need-Based Aid (FAFSA / Pell Grant)   │ (Up to $7,395 / year)
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

When layered together, these funding sources frequently exceed total tuition expenses, freeing teacher candidates to complete their clinical hours and student teaching without crushing student loan debt.

---

## The 4 Pillars of Stackable Funding

### 1. Federal & State Need-Based Aid (FAFSA)
*   **Federal Pell Grant:** Need-based federal grant that does not need to be repaid. Provides up to **$7,395/year** for eligible undergraduates.
*   **College Access Program (CAP) Grant:** State grant awarded by the Kentucky Higher Education Assistance Authority (KHEAA) to Pell-eligible Kentucky residents attending college. Provides up to **$5,300/year**.

### 2. Teacher-Specific Scholarships & Forgivable Loans
*   **KHEAA Teacher Scholarship:**
    *   *Award Amount:* Up to **$2,500/semester ($5,000/year)** for undergraduate education majors; up to $5,000/year for graduate students.
    *   *Requirement:* Kentucky resident admitted to a teacher education program who agrees to teach in an accredited Kentucky elementary or secondary school one semester for each semester of aid received.
*   **Federal TEACH Grant (Teacher Education Assistance for College and Higher Education):**
    *   *Award Amount:* Up to **$4,000/year** for undergraduate and graduate candidates.
    *   *Requirement:* Candidate must maintain a 3.25 GPA and sign an agreement to serve as a full-time, highly qualified teacher in a high-need field (e.g., Mathematics, Science, Special Education, Foreign Language, Reading Specialist) at a low-income school for at least 4 academic years within 8 years of graduation.
*   **Kentucky Teacher Recruitment Loan Program (TRLP):**
    *   *Award Amount:* Up to **$5,000/year** forgivable loans administered by KHEAA to recruit diverse and talented candidates into Kentucky classrooms.

### 3. Kentucky Educational Excellence Scholarship (KEES)
*   Earned by Kentucky high school students based on annual GPA and ACT/SAT scores.
*   Transfers directly to WKU to offset tuition, typically providing **$1,000 to $2,500/year** for high-achieving Kentucky high school graduates.

### 4. WKU University & CEBS Foundation Scholarships
*   **WKU Academic Merit Scholarships:** Awarded automatically to incoming freshmen based on high school GPA (ranging from $2,500 to full tuition).
*   **College of Education and Behavioral Sciences Endowed Scholarships:** Specific endowed scholarships awarded annually by CEBS to junior and senior teacher education majors during their clinical student teaching semesters.

## Four-Step Funding Roadmap for Students

1.  **File the FAFSA Early:** Submit the Free Application for Federal Student Aid at [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov) using WKU School Code: **002002**.
2.  **Apply for WKU Scholarships:** Complete the TopDollar scholarship application through WKU Financial Aid by February 1.
3.  **Apply for KHEAA Teacher Scholarship:** Submit the Teacher Scholarship application via [kheaa.com](https://www.kheaa.com) between February and May.
4.  **Complete the TEACH Grant Counseling:** If eligible, complete TEACH Grant counseling and Agreement to Serve (ATS) through WKU Student Financial Assistance.
