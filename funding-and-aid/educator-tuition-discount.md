# Graduate Educator Tuition Discount ($350/Credit Hour)

*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

To support practicing educators and invest directly in P-12 student achievement across the Commonwealth and region, Western Kentucky University has established the **Graduate Educator Tuition Discount**. Under this initiative, WKU has drastically reduced the cost of graduate education to a flat rate of **$350 per credit hour** for eligible licensed educators.

> *“This program builds on WKU’s strong history of educating educators and helping them prepare students—our most precious resource—to be the future of our Commonwealth.”*  
> — **Dr. Corinne M. Murphy**, Dean, College of Education and Behavioral Sciences

---

## Core Discount Terms & Eligibility

| Policy Feature | Provision Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Discounted Tuition Rate** | **$350.00 per graduate credit hour** *(Over 50% reduction off standard out-of-state and graduate tuition)* |
| **Commitment Period** | Active and approved through the **2029–2030 academic year** |
| **In-State Eligibility** | Legal residents of **Kentucky** holding an active Educator License issued by the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board (EPSB). |
| **Border-State Eligibility** | Legal residents of seven bordering states holding an active Educator License issued by their state department of education: **Illinois, Indiana, Missouri, Ohio, Tennessee, Virginia, and West Virginia**. |
| **Mandatory Condition** | **MUST submit a copy of an active teaching certificate** for each graduate application on file. |

## Step-by-Step Guide to Receive the Discount

### Step 1: Apply to The WKU Graduate School
*   Submit an electronic application through the WKU Graduate School portal ([apply.wku.edu](https://apply.wku.edu/portal/login)).
*   You may apply as either **degree-seeking** (e.g., [[programs/graduate/advanced-teacher-education-mae-0500|MAE 0500]], [[programs/graduate/literacy-education-mae-044|MAE 044]], [[programs/graduate/libraries-informatics-technology-ms-0497|MS LITE 0497]]) or **non-degree seeking** (e.g., certification add-on, Rank I planned program).

### Step 2: Submit Your Teaching Certificate
*   Email a digital PDF copy of your active, state-issued Educator License / Teaching Certificate directly to:  
    📧 **[graduate.school@wku.edu](mailto:graduate.school@wku.edu)**
*   Subject line: *"Educator Tuition Discount Certificate Submission - [Your Name] - [WKU ID]"*
*   Request confirmation that the discounted rate code has been applied to your student billing profile.

### Step 3: Verify Status in TopNet
*   Log into [TopNet](https://topnet.wku.edu/).
*   Navigate to: `Student Services` > `Student Records` > `View Graduate Program Documents`.
*   Confirm that your current teaching certificate is recorded as "Approved" and active.

### Step 4: Verify Student Billing Account
*   Review your e-bill through TopNet or contact the Office of Student Billing & Account Services:
    *   Email: [billing@wku.edu](mailto:billing@wku.edu) | Phone: (270) 745-6381.
    *   Confirm your tuition is assessed at $350/credit hour rather than standard graduate rates.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Does the discount apply to online courses?**  
A: Yes! The $350 rate applies to online, hybrid, and face-to-face courses in eligible programs.

**Q: Are Option 6 alternative certification candidates eligible?**  
A: **Yes.** Once the Kentucky EPSB issues your Temporary Provisional Teaching Certificate and it is submitted to the Graduate School, you qualify for the $350 rate as an employed educator of record.

**Q: Do out-of-state teachers in border states pay more?**  
A: **No.** Legal residents of IL, IN, MO, OH, TN, VA, and WV who hold valid state teacher licenses pay the exact same **$350 flat rate**, completely waiving standard non-resident tuition surcharges.
