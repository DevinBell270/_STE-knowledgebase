# Teacher Education Scholarships and Grants Directory

*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

This directory details specialized scholarship and grant opportunities available to undergraduate and graduate teacher candidates enrolled in the School of Teacher Education.

---

## State and Federal Teacher Aid Programs

| Award Program | Sponsor | Annual Amount | Eligibility & Service Commitment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **KHEAA Teacher Scholarship** | State of Kentucky | Up to **$5,000 / year** ($2,500/semester) | Kentucky resident admitted to Teacher Education; 1 semester of Kentucky public teaching service required per semester of funding. |
| **Federal TEACH Grant** | U.S. Dept. of Education | Up to **$4,000 / year** | 3.25 cumulative GPA; commit to teach full-time 4 years in a high-need field at a low-income school within 8 years of graduation. |
| **Kentucky Teacher Recruitment Loan (TRLP)** | State of Kentucky | Up to **$5,000 / year** | Forgivable loan recruiting underrepresented candidates into high-shortage school districts. |
| **Kentucky National Guard Tuition Assistance** | Commonwealth of KY | 100% In-State Tuition | Active members of the Kentucky National Guard in good standing. |

## CEBS Endowed & Foundation Scholarships
Applications for CEBS Foundation scholarships are submitted annually between November 1 and February 1 through WKU's **TopDollar** scholarship portal.

### Key Endowed Awards for Teacher Candidates:
*   **The Kelly Autism Program Clinical Scholarship:** For graduate candidates in SPED and ABA working directly with clients at the Suzanne Vitale CEC.
*   **The Carl & Mary Cooper Memorial Education Scholarship:** For undergraduate elementary education majors demonstrating financial need and high academic promise.
*   **The Dr. Julia Roberts Gifted Education Scholarship:** For graduate educators pursuing gifted education endorsements.
*   **The SKyTeach STEM Teaching Award:** Merit-based stipends for math and science majors completing clinical semesters.
*   **The Mary Jo & Jerry E. Baker Teacher Education Scholarship:** Awarded to clinical student teachers from South-Central Kentucky.
