## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Jennifer P. Klemm serves as Associate Dean in the College of Education and Behavioral Sciences. She coordinates college-level academic operations, curricular proposals through the Professional Education Council (PEC), student academic complaints, and accreditation compliance.
