## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Julia Link Roberts is the Mahurin Professor of Gifted Studies at WKU, Executive Director of The Center for Gifted Studies, and Executive Director of the Carol Martin Gatton Academy of Mathematics and Science. An internationally acclaimed scholar in gifted education, she has authored numerous seminal books on talent development, differentiation, and advocacy, and served on the Executive Committee of the World Council for Gifted and Talented Children.
