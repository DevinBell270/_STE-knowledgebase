## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Julia A. Mittelberg is an Associate Professor of Middle and Secondary Education. Her research and teaching center on inquiry-based social studies pedagogy, democratic citizenship education, and secondary teacher candidate development.

---

## Areas of Expertise & Research Interests

*   Secondary social studies education, historical thinking skills, civic engagement pedagogy, and middle grades curriculum.
