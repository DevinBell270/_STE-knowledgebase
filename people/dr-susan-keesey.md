## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Susan Keesey serves as the Director of the School of Teacher Education at Western Kentucky University. In this capacity, she provides executive, academic, and operational leadership for all undergraduate and graduate teacher preparation programs, clinical partnerships, accreditation reviews, and faculty development within the School.
