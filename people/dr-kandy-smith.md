## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Kandy C. Smith is an Associate Professor of Literacy in STE. She teaches graduate and undergraduate courses in reading diagnosis, content-area literacy strategies, and children's/adolescent literature.

---

## Areas of Expertise & Research Interests

*   Reading assessment, structured literacy interventions, writing instruction, and clinical teacher preparation.
