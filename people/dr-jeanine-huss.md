## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Jeanine M. Huss is a Professor of Elementary Education in the School of Teacher Education. She coordinates the Environmental Education P-12 endorsement and certificate programs, integrating environmental literacy, outdoor learning, and sustainability science into elementary teacher education.

---

## Areas of Expertise & Research Interests

*   Environmental education, elementary science methods, place-based education, sustainability education, and qualitative educational inquiry.
