# Dr. Rebecca R. Stobaugh

*Professor of Teacher Education*  
*School of Teacher Education*  
*College of Education and Behavioral Sciences*  
*Western Kentucky University*

---

## Academic Credentials & Background
*   **Ph.D. in Curriculum and Instruction:** University of Louisville (2003)
*   **M.A. in Secondary Education:** University of Kentucky
*   **B.A. in English and History Education:** Georgetown College

---

## Areas of Expertise & Scholarship
*   **The WKU Co-Teaching Model:** Author and presenter of WKU's foundational clinical video training series, *The Seven Methods of Co-Teaching*, utilized to train student teachers and P-12 cooperating teachers across Kentucky.
*   **Instructional Strategies & Cognitive Engagement:** Nationally recognized author of books on designing higher-level thinking tasks, cognitive engagement, and formative assessment.
*   **Critical Thinking & Teacher Leadership:** Core faculty developer for teacher leadership assessment sequences and performance-based rubrics.

---

## Clinical Co-Teaching Leadership
Dr. Stobaugh developed the instructional framework defining the seven methods of co-teaching implemented by the [[opes/overview-and-staff|Office of Professional Educator Services (OPES)]] in [[opes/co-teaching-model|WKU Co-Teaching Model]]:
1.  One Teach, One Observe
2.  One Teach, One Assist
3.  Station Teaching
4.  Parallel Teaching
5.  Supplemental Teaching
6.  Alternative / Differentiated Teaching
7.  Team Teaching
