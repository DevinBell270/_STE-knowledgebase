## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Erin E. Margarella is an Associate Professor of Middle and Secondary Education in STE. She teaches secondary English education methods, young adult literature, and instructional strategies for diverse secondary classrooms.

## Areas of Expertise & Research Interests
