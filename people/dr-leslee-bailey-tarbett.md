## Professional Role & Biography

Dr. Leslee Bailey Tarbett is an Assistant Professor of Special Education. Her scholarly focus includes transition planning for youth with disabilities, universal design for learning (UDL), and collaborative school models.

## Areas of Expertise & Research Interests
