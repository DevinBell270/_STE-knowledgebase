## Your Journey to the Classroom Starts Here

A career in teaching empowers you to ignite curiosity, foster growth, and shape the future, one student at a time. At Western Kentucky University's School of Teacher Education, we provide a clear, supportive, and hands-on path to get you from where you are today into a classroom where you can thrive.

### A WKU Degree Gets You Hired

We give you the experience, skills, and support to step into your first classroom with confidence.

### Hilltopper Stories

Dive into the vibrant experiences and inspiring journeys of our Hilltopper community through firsthand accounts and testimonials, showcasing the unparalleled student life and success stories.

#### Mikayla Gambrel

"I want to be a teacher because teaching is a wonderful way to make an impact on children, and I hope to be that positive influence our next generation needs. I chose ELA because reading and writing is a great way to understand the world and let the world understand you, and I want to share my love for literature with students."

#### Jayla Sharp

"I want to be a teacher because I find so much joy in building meaningful relationships with students from so many different backgrounds. I want to help all students that I come across grow academically and personally in an environment that is comfortable and safe, so that these students feel supported and loved. My goal is to make a lasting positive impact on so many young lives."
