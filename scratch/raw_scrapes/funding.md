# How WKU Education Majors Cover the Full Cost of Tuition

* * *

## Have Your WKU Teaching Degree Fully Funded

Thanks to stackable scholarships and aid, you can see your tuition fully funded, giving you the freedom to pursue your calling without financial burden.

See How It Works →

### How "Stackable" Aid Covers Your Tuition

At WKU, aspiring teachers can take advantage of scholarships and grants that, when combined with federal aid, often offset the full cost of tuition. The key is "stacking" multiple sources of funding.

#### 

#### **Federal & State Aid (FAFSA)**

Filing the FAFSA is a critical step for federal and state financial aid:

  * [Pell Grant →](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/grants/pell)
  * [CAP Grant →](https://www.kheaa.com/web/scholarships-grants.faces#collegeAccess)



#### 

#### **Teacher-Specific Aid**

These programs are designed specifically for future Kentucky teachers:

  * [Teacher Recruitment (TRLP) →](https://www.kheaa.com/web/scholarships-grants.faces#teacher_recruit_ln_pgm)
  * [KHEAA Teacher Scholarship →](https://www.kheaa.com/web/scholarships-grants.faces#teacher)
  * [TEACH Grant →](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/grants/teach)



#### 

#### **WKU & State Scholarships**

These university and state awards recognize your academic achievements:

  * [WKU Academic Scholarships →](/financialaid/scholarships/)
  * [KEES Scholarship →](https://www.kheaa.com/web/scholarships-grants.faces#kees)

### Your 3 Simple Next Steps

Ready to become a Hilltopper teacher with your tuition covered? Here's how to start.

**1.**

#### **Apply to WKU**

Get started on your application to join the Hilltopper family. Our admissions team is here to help.

[Apply Now →](https://apply.wku.edu/portal/login)

**2.**

#### **File Your FAFSA**

This is the key! The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) unlocks most of the grants shown above. Do this as soon as possible.

[Go to FAFSA.gov →](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa)

**3.**

#### **Apply for Teacher Scholarships**

Visit the KHEAA and WKU scholarship websites to apply for aid specifically for education majors.

[School Of Teacher Education Scholarship Opportunities →](/becomeateacher/teaching-scholarships.php)  
[KHEAA Teacher Scholarship →](https://www.kheaa.com/website/kheaa/teacher_scholarship?main=1)  
[TopDollar →](/financialaid/scholarships/)
