# Options Available in 4 Colleges

* * *

The WKU Educator Discount is currently available in [College of Education and Behavioral Sciences](/cebs/), [College of Health and Human Services](/chhs/), [Gordon Ford College of Business](/business/ "Gordon Ford College of Business"), and [Potter College of Arts and Letters](/pcal/).

## [College of Education and Behavioral Sciences](/cebs/)

The College of Education and Behavioral Sciences is home to WKU's nationally recognized clinical preparation programs. These programs focus on our community's future teachers, counselors, psychologists, and leaders.

[ View Eligible Programs](/educatordiscount/cebs_eligible_programs.php)

## [College of Health and Human Services](/chhs/)

Our vision is to be an innovative leader in the student-centered, interprofessional, preparation of health and human services professionals by providing students with an inclusive, community-engaged, and interdisciplinary academic experience.

[ View Eligible Programs](/educatordiscount/chhs_eligible_programs.php)

## [Gordon Ford College of Business](/business/)

The Gordon Ford College of Business is home to six departments which offer robust undergraduate and graduate programs, certificates, and minors. GFCB's programs and student success activities provide students the foundation to lead in today's diverse business environments.

## [Potter College of Arts and Letters](/pcal/)

PCAL is home to the visual and performing arts, the humanities, and the social sciences. Our departments include Art & Design, Communication, English, Folk Studies and Anthropology, History, Modern Languages, Music, Political Science, Sociology & Criminology, Theatre and Dance as well as the School of Media.

[ View Eligible Programs](/educatordiscount/pcal_eligible_programs.php)
