### Course Spotlight: Your Learning Journey

Get a glimpse into the types of engaging courses you'll take in our Elementary Education program. Here are a few examples:

#### **Foundations of Elementary Teaching**

**ELED 345 | 3 Credit Hours**

Build your core teaching skills! This course connects learning theories with practical planning and evaluation techniques for diverse student needs. Includes required field experience.

#### **Teaching Methods for Elementary Teachers**

**ELED 365 | 3 Credit Hours**

Learn effective teaching strategies specifically designed for integrated elementary classrooms. Apply what you learn through required field experiences in schools.

#### **Teaching Science in the Elementary School**

**ELED 406 | 3 Credit Hours**

Discover how to effectively teach science concepts (grades P-5). This course covers planning, using materials, and developing engaging science teaching techniques, enhanced by field experience.

#### **Student Teaching**

**ELED 490 | 5-10 Credit Hours**

Put your learning into practice! This supervised, immersive experience places you in an approved school setting for a minimum of 70 days, preparing you for your own classroom.

### Explore Our Elementary Education Programs

Discover the pathway that fits your career goals in elementary education (P-5). We offer programs for aspiring undergraduates, those seeking initial certification via a master's degree, and experienced educators pursuing advanced credentials.

Undergraduate Program

**Elementary Education, Bachelor of Science (B.S.)**  
For students seeking their initial Kentucky teaching certificate (P-5) as part of a bachelor's degree.  
[Learn More: B.S. Program](https://catalog.wku.edu/undergraduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/elementary-education-bs/)

Graduate Programs

**Teacher Education, Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT)**  
For individuals with a non-education bachelor's degree seeking initial P-5 teaching certification.  
[Learn More: MAT Program](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/teacher-ed-initial-cert-mat/)

**Advanced Teacher Education, Master of Arts in Education (MAE)**  
For certified teachers seeking advanced knowledge and professional development.  
[Learn More: MAE Program](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/advanced-teacher-education-mae/)

**Elementary Education Rank I**  
This non-degree program leads to eligibility for Rank I.  
[Learn More: Rank I](https://catalog.wku.edu/graduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/non-degree-planned-sixth-year-elementary-rI/)

**Elementary Math Specialization P-5, Certificate**  
This P-5 certificate equips education professionals with deep elementary mathematics knowledge and collaborative skills needed to support effective instruction and professional learning.
