# Teacher Education Program Admissions

* * *

## Admissions Procedures

Welcome to the Professional Educator Services admissions process. Follow the
steps below to ensure you meet all requirements for admission into the
program.

* * *

### Step 1 - Professional Education Admissions Orientation Meetings

All students who wish to enter WKU’s Professional Educational program must
attend a mandatory Orientation Session. This is also a major requirement
before being assigned to any school district to complete clinical fieldwork
for all School of Teacher Education courses.

_Attendance will be taken at each session and reported to the instructors of
introductory courses._

### Step 2 - Tasks To Complete Prior To Orientation

Complete the following tasks before your scheduled orientation session:

  * Complete Medical Physical/TB Screening (TB skin test only needed if exam indicates risk factors) on the required [KY Department of Education Medical Examination of School Employee form](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10VM1cWFYGMpq3lC5L9kUkKHPJ5wUdMbC/view).
  * Complete Criminal Background Check report completed from [Verified Credentials Agency](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1N1hBdAZmGG-1_08RPwhHgUyzjtcOw5yMUnrn-pI-1hc/edit?tab=t.0).
  * Complete CAN (Child Abuse & Neglect) check from [CAN Check](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MJd2x_FTcAgZ-05EQXNd0mKjFjJRrT4XuFdE5d0WO18/edit?usp=sharing).
  * If you previously attended an orientation and did not receive a Teacher Education ID badge please email a professional looking headshot along with your campus location to [**teacher.services@wku.edu**](mailto:teacher.services@wku.edu). You will receive an email notification when your badge is ready for pick up.

All background check documents need to be uploaded to your Anthology account.
You will learn how to create your account at the teacher admissions
orientation. If you have not attended an orientation you may register below.

### Step 3 - Academic Standing and Testing

You must also meet the following academic and testing requirements:

  * Achieve at least an overall GPA of 2.75
  * Submit Passing Praxis CORE ([Core Academic Skills for Educator exams](https://praxis.ets.org/))
    * Reading minimum passing score: **150**
    * Writing minimum passing score: **158**
    * Math minimum passing score: **144**

#### **Please Note**

WKU (code #1901) and the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board
[KYEPSB](https://goteachky.com/) (code #7283) should both be listed as score
recipients when you register for the exams. ETS charges a $50 fee to send the
scores if score recipients are not listed at the time of registration.
