# WKU Co-Teaching

* * *

Thank you for agreeing to supervise a WKU student teacher. Per Kentucky
Regulations [16 KAR
5:040](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/016/005/040.pdf), cooperating
teachers and university supervisors must complete training on their basic
responsibilities prior to supervising a student teacher.

The training is composed of three parts (A, B, and C), which are detailed
below.

### Important Notice for Cooperating Teachers

**Effective July 1, 2019:** If you passed the Part A training via Edmodo
before July 1, 2019, you are not required to take the new assessment in iDrive
Digital.

* * *

You can find more information on the new requirements at the [GoTeachKY
website](https://goteachky.com/resources/educator-preparation/student-
teaching-placements/cooperating-teacher-training/).

## Required Training Steps

Part A: Basic Responsibilities (iDrive Quiz)

This online training and assessment is provided by the KY Department of
Education’s Office of Educator Licensure and Effectiveness (OELE).

  * [Launch Part A Training (iDrive Digital)](https://otis.coe.uky.edu/DDL/launch.php)
  * [View Directions for Creating an iDrive Digital Account](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dNlbhx_pp-HPNsypNtFAAy2A4VSe2sST/view)

Part B: Best Practice in Supporting a Student Teacher

Part B is a training video designed to prepare you for your supervisory role.
After watching the video, you must send an email to
[sarah.fischer@wku.edu](mailto:sarah.fischer@wku.edu) to confirm completion.

[Watch Part B Video](http://www.epsb.ky.gov/mod/page/view.php?id=476)

Part C: Effective Assessment of the Student Teacher

Please review the WKU Student Teaching Handbook to familiarize yourself with
the required forms and responsibilities for effective assessment.

[Review WKU Student Teaching
Handbook](https://sites.google.com/g.wku.edu/studentteachingsite/mentor-
teachers)
