## Alternative Pathways to Certification

### **Start Your Teaching Journey Today!**

Western Kentucky University provides an alternate way to recognize competency
and proficiency for individuals with a bachelor’s degree and relevant school-
based experience, this program lets you earn your teaching license faster by
showcasing your existing skills.

[Talk to a Certification Advisor](/alternative-teacher-
certification/contact.php)

_This process aligns with**16 KAR 5:030 on Proficiency Evaluation** and offers
an option beyond academic credit to demonstrate professional readiness._

### Is This Pathway Right for You?

This program is designed for individuals who already have a strong foundation
from their education and work experience. See if you meet the core
requirements to get started.

  * Bachelor’s Degree from an Accredited Institution.

  * GPA of 2.75+ (or 3.0 in last 30 hours).

  * Relevant school-based work experience.

#### **Unsure if You Qualify?**

[ Contact Us](/alternative-teacher-certification/contact.php)

### Your 4-Step Path to the Classroom

Step 1:

Submit Your Digital Portfolio

Compile your achievements, professional experience, and teaching skills. This
is your chance to showcase your knowledge and competence.

Step 2:

Receive a Personalized Plan

Our committee will review your portfolio and create a tailored Educator
Learning Plan (ELP) outlining your specific next steps.

Step 3:

Get Your Provisional Certificate

Once your ELP is approved, you'll receive a provisional teaching certificate,
allowing you to begin applying for teaching positions right away.

Step 4:

Start Teaching!

Begin your teaching career while completing the final requirements outlined in
your ELP, blending professional development with real-world experience.

## Application Deadlines & Decision Dates

To be considered for Proficiency Evaluation, candidates must submit all
application documents, fees, and portfolio evidence by the submission deadline
below. Late applications will need to wait for the next review cycle.

**Semester** | **Submission Deadline** | **Committee Decision**  
---|---|---  
**Fall** | October 1st | December 15th  
**Spring** | March 1st | May 15th  
**Summer** | June 1st | August 1st
