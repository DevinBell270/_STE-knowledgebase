# Office of Professional Educator Services

* * *

## Inspire Every Student

### Become a Teacher with WKU's Office of Professional Educator Services

The Office of Professional Educator Services empowers tomorrow’s teachers by
providing expert guidance through hands-on experience, student teaching, and
certification, so every student can thrive.

## Your Path to Becoming a Teacher

A step-by-step guide for students in the Office of Professional Educator
Services.

### **Step 1:** Admission to Professional Education

This is your official entry into the teacher education program. It shows you
have the academic foundation to become a successful educator.

[Learn more about Teacher
Admissions](/educatorservices/teacher_admissions/index.php)

### **Step 2:** Gain Field Experience

Get 200 hours of hands-on experience in diverse K-12 classrooms. You'll
observe, participate, and connect theory with practice.

[Explore Field Experience
opportunities](/educatorservices/field_exp/index.php)

### **Step 3:** Complete Student Teaching

Apply your skills during a full-semester, immersive experience. You'll work
with a mentor teacher to plan lessons and manage a classroom.

[Prepare for your Student Teaching
semester](/educatorservices/student_teaching/index.php)

### **Step 4:** Teacher Certification

After graduating, apply for your official state teaching license. Our office
will guide you through the final process and exams.

[Get started on Teacher
Certification](/educatorservices/teacher_cert/index.php)

### Hilltopper Stories

Dive into the vibrant experiences and inspiring journeys of our Hilltopper
community through firsthand accounts and testimonials, showcasing the

#### Mikayla Gambrel

"I want to be a teacher because teaching is a wonderful way to make an impact
on children, and I hope to be that positive influence our next generation
needs. I chose ELA because reading and writing is a great way to understand
the world and let the world understand you, and I want to share my love for
literature with students."

#### Jayla Sharp

"I want to be a teacher because I find so much joy in building meaningful
relationships with students from so many different backgrounds. I want to help
all students that I come across grow academically and personally in an
environment that is comfortable and safe, so that these students feel
supported and loved. My goal is to make a lasting positive impact on so many
young lives."
