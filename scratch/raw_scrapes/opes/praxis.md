# Praxis Test Preparation

* * *

## Important Praxis Test Information

The Praxis® tests are a series of exams required by the Kentucky Education
Professional Standards Board for individuals entering an educator preparation
program and seeking certification to teach. These exams measure the academic
skills and subject-specific content knowledge necessary for a career in
education. This page outlines the specific Praxis requirements and resources
available to WKU students.

### **Please Note: At Home Testing Scores**

Praxis Tests taken using the **At Home option will not automatically report
scores.** Score recipients must be added for all Praxis exam test takers.

  * Test Takers will need to list the state agency - **Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board (code 7283)** as one of the free score recipients.
  * **Western Kentucky University** will also need to be added as a score recipient.

Both of these recipients can be added for free at the time of testing.

### **Pending Praxis Scores in EPSB**

When logging into EPSB, if you see your Praxis scores are pending, you will
need to follow the instructions below to resolve this issue:

  * **If you took the test(s) out-of-state** and did not request the scores to be sent to EPSB, please log on to ETS.org and request that your scores be sent to EPSB (Education Professional Standards Board). You will need to use their state code #7283 to add them as a score recipient when you make your request. ETS charges a $50 fee for this request.
  * **If you took the test(s) in-state,** please contact KDE Office of Education, Licensure & Effectiveness at 1-888-598-7667, press “0”, and ask for Professional Learning Assessments to verify that your social security number is correct in their database, as well as on your Praxis score report.

### **ETS Resources**

  * [Praxis Educator Preparation Entry Assessments](https://praxis.ets.org/state-requirements/kentucky-tests.html)
  * [At Home Testing Option information](https://praxis.ets.org/test-takers/test-day-at-home.html)

#### **Important: Avoid a $50 Fee on Your Praxis Scores**

Pending scores will impact your teaching certification eligibility. To prevent
delays and fees, you **must** list **both** of the following as score
recipients when you take your exam:

  * **KY-EPSB** (Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board)

  * **Western Kentucky University**

If your scores must be requested later, ETS charges a **$50 fee for _each_
score**.

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

Praxis Exams: Frequently Asked QuestionsWhich test(s) do I need to take?

This depends on your goal. There are two main types of Praxis exams:

  * **For Admission into Teacher Education:** You need the **Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators** exam.

  * **For Teacher Certification (Licensing):** You need the **Praxis Subject Assessments** , which are specific to your teaching area.

To find the exact tests and passing scores required in Kentucky, visit the
[ETS Kentucky Requirements page](https://praxis.ets.org/state-
requirements/kentucky-tests.html).

[See requirements for all other states](https://praxis.ets.org/state-
requirements.html)

How do I register for a test?

All registration is handled directly by the Educational Testing Service (ETS).
**You cannot register through any WKU office.**

You can register in one of three ways:

  * **Online (Recommended):**[Visit the ETS Praxis registration page](http://www.ets.org/praxis/register).

  * By Phone

  * By Mail

How much does the test cost?

Costs vary depending on which test(s) you select. ETS provides a complete,
itemized list of fees.

[See all Praxis test fees](http://www.ets.org/praxis/about/fees)

Is there any fee assistance available?

Yes. If you are eligible for financial aid, you may qualify for a fee waiver
from ETS.

The waiver can cover the fees for:

  * Up to three (3) Praxis Core tests, **OR**

  * One (1) Praxis Subject Assessment

To learn more and see if you qualify, please [review the ETS fee waiver
information
sheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-mdaWsuT1DoYLNLINkLCqtBU7Io6l8NSBbRt_AnGo30/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.e1m7jnbrxov5).

Where do I take the test?

All Praxis tests are computer-based and must be taken at a designated testing
center.

  * **Testing at WKU:** Many Praxis exams are offered on campus at the **WKU Counseling & Testing Center** (Potter Hall, Room 409).

  * **Other Locations:** You can take the test at any official testing center in the country.

[Find test centers and see which tests are
offered](http://www.ets.org/praxis/register/centers_dates/)

When is the test offered?

This depends on the specific test. Some are offered continuously, while others
are only available during specific "testing windows" throughout the year.

[Find specific test dates](http://www.ets.org/praxis/register/centers_dates/)

Are there accommodations for disabilities or non-native English speakers?

Yes. ETS provides accommodations for test takers who meet specific
requirements.

Examples include:

  * Extended testing time

  * Additional breaks

  * An audio version of the test

  * A separate testing room

For a full list of accommodations and instructions on how to apply, [visit the
ETS accommodations page](http://www.ets.org/praxis/register/disabilities/).

What do I need to bring on test day?

**What to Bring:**

  * Your printed admission ticket

  * A valid photo ID (such as a driver's license or passport)

**Important:** Your **WKU Student ID is NOT** an acceptable form of ID for
this test.

**What to Check:**

  * **Calculators** are only permitted for _some_ tests. Check the policy for your specific exam.

  * Personal items (phones, bags, etc.) are generally not allowed in the testing room.

For the complete list of rules, [review the official ETS test day
policies](http://www.ets.org/praxis/test_day/).

How can I contact ETS?

ETS can be reached by phone, email, fax, or mail.

[See all ETS contact options](http://www.ets.org/praxis/contact)
