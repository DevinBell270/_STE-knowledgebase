# Undergraduate Teaching Programs at WKU’s School of Teacher Education

* * *

## Explore Undergraduate Teaching Programs at WKU

Start your journey to becoming a certified K–12 teacher with one of WKU’s undergraduate programs in the School of Teacher Education. Whether you’re passionate about early childhood, elementary, middle grades, special education, or STEM subjects, we offer hands-on, high-impact pathways that prepare you for success in today’s classrooms.

### **Elementary Education, Bachelor of Science**

School of Teacher Education, Western Kentucky University

Prepare to teach in elementary school settings with a strong foundation in child development, curriculum planning, and classroom management. Ideal for future K–5 educators.

**Program Code:** 527

[Program Info](/ste/eled.php)

### **Interdisciplinary Early Childhood Education, Bachelor of Science**

School of Teacher Education, Western Kentucky University

Designed for those who want to work with children from birth through kindergarten. Combines developmental theory with practical teaching experience in early learning environments.

**Program Code:** 526

[Program Info](/ste/iece.php)

### **Middle Level Education in Social Studies and Language Arts, Bachelor of Science**

**School of Teacher Education, Western Kentucky University**

Focus on teaching grades 5–9 with subject-area specialization in language arts and social studies. Gain strategies for engaging adolescents in critical thinking and communication.

**Program Code:** 5001

[Program Info](/ste/mged.php)

### **Science and Mathematics Education (SMED), Bachelor of Science**

School of Teacher Education, Western Kentucky University

Specialized STEM teaching track for those interested in middle or high school science or math. Emphasizes inquiry-based instruction, mentoring, and educational research.

**Program Code:** 774

[Program Info](/skyteach/)

### **Special Education: Learning and Behavior Disorders and Elementary Education, Bachelor of Science**

### School of Teacher Education, Western Kentucky University

Dual certification program preparing you to teach both in elementary classrooms and in special education settings. Focus on inclusive practices and individualized instruction.

**Program Code:** 5003

[Program Info](/ste/sped.php)

### **Interactive Training and Leadership, Certificate**

School of Teacher Education, Western Kentucky University

This certificate program builds skills in instructional design, training development, and organizational leadership. A great supplement for aspiring educators or trainers.

**Program Code:** 1725

[Program Info](https://catalog.wku.edu/undergraduate/education-behavioral-sciences/teacher-education/interactive-training-design-certificate/)
