### Shape Your Future in Education at WKU

Start your journey to becoming an impactful teacher. At WKU's School of Teacher Education, we provide a direct path to a successful and rewarding career. Our programs are designed to get you into real P-12 classrooms, learning by doing.

#### 

#### **Gain Hands-On Experience From the Start**

At WKU, you won’t just learn how to teach, you’ll start teaching right away. From your first courses, you'll engage in clinical field experiences in local P-12 schools, culminating in a full-time student teaching semester.

#### ****

#### **Learn From Expert, Supportive Faculty**

Our faculty are dedicated mentors with deep expertise in areas like classroom management and student diversity. You'll receive personalized guidance every step of the way through a structured advising system to help you achieve your goals.

#### ****

#### **Earn a Nationally Accredited Degree**

Your WKU degree signals excellence to employers nationwide. Our school is nationally accredited, meaning our programs meet the highest standards and provide a clear, respected pathway to teacher certification.
