# Graduate Educator Tuition Discount for Kentucky & Border State Teachers

* * *

## Graduate Educator Tuition Discount

We have reduced the rate of graduate education for In-state and Border-state licensed educators to $350/credit hour for the 2020 - 2030 academic year.

Get the DiscountCheck Eligibility →

### Supporting Our Educators

Current Educators continue to work harder than ever. WKU wants to thank you for the exceptional work you do in the classroom ensuring that students of the Commonwealth continue to learn through this extraordinary time.

#### **Not a Current Licensed Educator?**

Thanks to stackable scholarships and aid, you can see your tuition fully funded. [Learn More About Stackable Aid.](/ste/teacher-funding.php)

### Who is Eligible?

Select your residency status to see details.

  * [View Eligible Courses](/educatordiscount/eligible_programs.php)
  * [View Eligible Programs](/educatordiscount/options.php)



#### 

#### **In-State Residents  
**

Legal residents of **Kentucky** who hold a current Kentucky Educator License issued by the Education Professional Standards Board (EPSB).

#### 

#### **Border State Residents**

Legal residents of a **bordering state*** who hold a current Educator License issued by their respective State Department of Education.

*Illinois, Indiana, Missouri, Ohio, Tennessee, Virginia, & West Virginia.

#### **Requirement Warning  
**

Eligibility **REQUIRES** submission of a current teaching certificate (Educator License) to the Graduate School. Students who do not submit the certificate will not receive the discounted rate. Students must upload a Teaching Certificate for each application on file.

### Your Step-by-Step Guide to Receive the Discount

1

#### **Apply to Graduate School**

Your first action is to complete the main application through The WKU Graduate School and review their admission requirements. First-time students new to WKU may apply to the Graduate School to enroll as either a degree-seeking or non-degree seeking student.

[Start Your WKU Graduate Application](https://apply.wku.edu/portal/login)

2

#### **Submit Teaching Certificate**

Submit your current EPSB issued Teaching Certificate to the Graduate School.

Email certificate to [graduate.school@wku.edu](mailto:graduate.school@wku.edu)

Request confirmation that the discount will be applied to your account.

3

#### **Confirm File Status**

Ensure your certificate is active and on file.

[Log into Topnet](https://acsapps.wku.edu/pls/prod/twbkwbis.P_WKULogin)

Student Services > Student Records > View Graduate Program Documents

4

#### **Check Application  
**

Monitor the status of your application.

[Check Application Status](https://apply.wku.edu/account/login?r=https://apply.wku.edu/portal/login)

### Frequently Asked Questions:

How do I know if I have a current teaching certificate on file with the Graduate School?

All approved matriculation forms are viewable on [TopNet](http://topnet.wku.edu/) ( _Click Student Services > Student Records > View Graduate Program Documents_ ). Please make sure the certificate date on file is currently active.

How do I sign and submit forms electronically to the Graduate School?

Save the form to your desktop, complete, and save. After obtaining the appropriate approvals, e-mail forms to [graduate.school@wku.edu](mailto:graduate.school@wku.edu). If you have technical issues please contact the WKU IT help desk,  
270-745-7000.
